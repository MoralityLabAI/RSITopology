# Iliad Fellowship research links

This page is a compact guide to Patrick Deegan's mathematical AI-alignment
work in `MoralityLabAI/RSITopology`. The repository combines exact finite
mathematics, preregistered synthetic controls, real-model falsification, and
tools for carrying geometric measurements into monitoring and model-edit
decisions.

The work is deliberately scoped. The AI Safety Millennium Problems (ASMPs) are
candidate research problems, not claimed prize problems; none is claimed
resolved. Synthetic holonomy controls validate instruments, not transformer
phenomena. Real-model null and boundary results are reported as such.

## Three links to start with

1. **[AI Safety Millennium Problems: definitions and resolution
   rules](ultra-experiments/millennium/AI_SAFETY_MILLENNIUM_PROBLEMS_v0_1.md)**

   Seven architecture-independent mathematical questions spanning mechanistic
   identifiability, distribution shift, weak verification, two-port control,
   reflective safety, collusion capacity, and capability attestation. Each
   problem includes positive and negative resolution obligations, a
   computability boundary, a safety reduction requirement, and an empirical
   firewall.

2. **[ASMP progress map and peer-review
   guide](ultra-experiments/millennium/PROBLEM_PROGRESS_SUMMARY_v0_1.md)**

   A conservative map from the broad questions to exact theorem seeds,
   registered finite experiments, and the remaining proof debt. It records
   that all seven original problems have bounded, problem-ID-bound work while
   zero are claimed resolved.

3. **[Quantization erodes the geometry of a rank-one identity object before it
   touches the
   topology](reports/QWEN08_PRECISION_GEOMETRY_PAPER_V0_1.md)**

   A Qwen-0.8B study of identity geometry under four-bit quantization. It
   reports support-margin compression and one support-gate crossing, rejects a
   fine-tuning-specific effect, and proves that the observed positive
   rank-one holonomy was geometrically forced. The resulting liveness theorem
   replaces a dead binary invariant with the graded frustration margin.

## Mathematical and empirical evidence

### Mechanistic identifiability

- **[Real-model projector tomography
  result](ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp1_qwen_projector_tomography/RESULT.md)**:
  identity-certified Qwen-0.8B directions were causally live, but a complete
  four-site intervention cube found no context-stable pairwise interaction
  algebra. This is a preregistered negative result that narrows the next object
  to context-conditioned interactions.

### Scalable oversight

- **[Exact weak-verifier
  frontier](ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp3_weak_verifier_frontier/RESULT_v0_1.md)**:
  exact rational calculations separate query count, error correlation, and
  verifier-family diversity. A 1,024-atom construction also shows that 99.9%
  atom-averaged accuracy can coexist with 100% failure on an adversarially
  selected refutation.

### Evaluator-relative control

- **[Exact two-port control phase
  map](ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp4_two_port_game/RESULT.md)**:
  a 108-cell rational census separates read-interface and write-interface
  insufficiency. In the registered architecture, total nominal capacity cannot
  compensate for eliminating either port.

### Value and reward identifiability

- **[ASMP-9 resolution audit after
  v0.28](ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/RESOLUTION_AUDIT_v0_28.md)**:
  a clause-by-clause audit of a long exact-mathematics program on reward gauge,
  ordinal access, finite-sample coherence, finite-MDP interventions, context
  gluing, graph design, offset calibration, calibrated occupancy access, and
  response-link misspecification. It distinguishes proved special cases from
  the remaining general obligations.

- **[Extended ASMP experiment
  progress](ultra-experiments/millennium/V0_2_EXPERIMENT_PROGRESS_v0_1.md)**:
  concise status for additional directions on Goodhart frontiers, reward
  gauges, capability transitions, bounded tiling, conditional defection, and
  program equilibria.

## Holonomy and identity-attestation stack

- **[The Missing Phase of Bundle
  Lineage](reports/monitor_holonomy_insight.md)**: the core synthetic
  construction. Two bundles have matched local worst-direction retention
  (`0.990033`), while one has zero loop rotation and the other accumulates a
  measured `62.173` degree holonomy. It includes the area-law control,
  perimeter-scaling estimation-noise null, orientation-reversal gate, and
  independent from-specification reproduction.

- **[Identity-attestation integration
  contract](docs/IDENTITY_ATTESTATION_INTEGRATION.md)**: an API-level bridge
  from basis hashes, edge lineage, loop closure, and determinant flags to
  authorization rules for monitors, invariant energy rewards, and signed
  model edits.

- **[Control-risk theorem](docs/CONTROL_RISK_THEOREM.md)** and **[registered
  red-team results](docs/CONTROL_RISK_REDTEAM_RESULTS.md)**: a conservative
  lineage-plus-holonomy bound for signed-coordinate identity loss, followed by
  a 6,000-chain synthetic red team with zero false authorizations at 13.2%
  selective coverage. The result is explicitly not a predictor of general
  self-improvement risk.

- **[Edit-sectioning crossover
  result](docs/EDIT_SECTIONING_V03_RESULTS.md)**: a calibrated demonstration
  that holonomy-bounded pooling helps when patch boundaries match edit identity
  and loses when those boundaries are deliberately decoupled. This prevents
  the method from claiming an advantage manufactured by its fixture.

- **[Goedel globe interactive receipt
  viewer](godel-globe/index.html)** and **[usage
  guide](godel-globe/README.md)**: a static Three.js interface for exploring
  edge lineage, loop holonomy, determinant breaks, certificates, and measured
  parallel transport. The spherical embedding is presentational; displayed
  angles come only from receipts.

## Suggested application framing

> I am developing gauge-invariant and information-theoretic tools for deciding
> when an AI-safety measurement continues to refer to the same mechanism across
> contexts, checkpoints, and model transformations. My work combines exact
> finite theorems, adversarially designed controls, and small real-model
> falsification experiments. The ASMP agenda states the long-horizon
> mathematical questions and two-sided resolution rules; the holonomy work is
> a concrete example of the research method, including a liveness theorem that
> explains when a seemingly stable topological result is actually
> non-informative.

## Reproducibility posture

The linked experiments preserve protocols, registrations, hashes, run
receipts, independent verifiers, resource ceilings, and explicit claim
boundaries where applicable. Exact finite results are kept separate from
statistical, synthetic, and real-model evidence.
