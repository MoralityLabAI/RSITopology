# Fellowship research portfolio: ASMPs and identity geometry

This page is a short review path through two connected parts of the
RSITopology program:

1. the AI Safety Millennium Problems (ASMPs), an initial seven-problem set
   with explicit resolution standards plus a prior-art-gated v0.2 expansion;
   and
2. identity geometry for mechanistic interpretability and control, including
   spectral lineage, loop holonomy, real-model falsification work, and an
   executable attestation layer.

The work is intentionally separated into exact finite mathematics, synthetic
instrument validation, and real-model evidence. None of the bounded results is
presented as a resolution of a full ASMP, and no result is evidence of
recursive self-improvement.

## Representative ASMP results

### Mechanistic identifiability

- [Interaction-order tomography theorem and census](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp1_interaction_tomography/RESULT_v0_1.md):
  exact finite results for which interaction orders can be recovered from
  bounded-order interventions, including a symmetry-quotiented threshold and
  an explicit separation between exact identifiability and numerical
  conditioning.
- [Qwen0.8B projector-tomography falsification](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp1_qwen_projector_tomography/RESULT.md):
  identity-certified directions have live causal magnitude, but the complete
  four-site intervention cube does not support a context-stable pairwise
  interaction algebra. The frozen verdict is
  `stable_nonadditivity_not_established`.

### Scalable oversight

- [Moment-order certificate for a weak-verifier panel](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp3_weak_verifier_frontier/MOMENT_ORDER_RESULT_v0_1_3.md):
  an exact primal-dual result showing that, for the frozen nine-judge
  exchangeable panel, marginal through third-order dependence information
  cannot certify a 5% majority-error target; fourth-order information is the
  first sufficient rung relative to the registered independent reference.

### Decision-relative value access

- [Finite sequential risk-access characterization](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/sequential_risk_access_v0_41/RESULT_v0_41.md):
  an exact Bellman-recursive characterization of the upper risk polytope
  generated by finite adaptive experiments. On the registered rational
  fixture, adaptive access has deficiency `61/135`, versus `13/25` for
  randomized open-loop access, while a second decision problem has no adaptive
  advantage.

These are bounded theorem seeds and falsification instruments. Their purpose is
to turn broad safety claims into checkable mathematical obligations, not to
rename finite examples as general solutions.

## Holonomy and identity-attestation work

### Mathematical idea

[The missing phase of bundle lineage](../reports/monitor_holonomy_insight.md)
shows why principal-angle similarity is insufficient for signed mechanistic
coordinates. Pairwise subspace overlap discards the orthogonal transport
factor. Those locally benign factors can compose around a loop into nontrivial
holonomy, so pairwise monitor handoffs can all look good while the transported
coordinate fails to have one global identity.

The report includes matched lower-level controls, a flat estimation-noise null,
canonical rotation-angle receipts, orientation-reversal handling through
`det(H)`, a frozen spanning-tree convention, and an independent
from-specification reproduction.

### Contact with a real model

[Quantization erodes the geometry of a rank-one identity object before it
touches the topology](../reports/QWEN08_PRECISION_GEOMETRY_PAPER_V0_1.md)
tests the stack on a frozen Qwen0.8B capture. Its most important methodological
result is a liveness theorem: for rank-one shortest-geodesic transport,
negative holonomy requires the sum of edge principal angles around a cycle to
reach at least 180 degrees. Every measured cycle was below that bound.
Consequently, all-positive loop signs were not counted as confirming stable
topology. The graded frustration margin, rather than the forced binary class,
is reported as the live diagnostic.

## Review posture

The common research habit across both tracks is to make the diagnostic capable
of failing before interpreting a pass. Protocols freeze gates before outcomes;
receipts bind code, inputs, and results; negative and unavailable outcomes are
retained; classical ingredients are attributed rather than relabeled as new
theory; and real-model nulls narrow the next object instead of being hidden.

The unifying question is:

> What mathematical evidence is sufficient to say that a measured feature,
> evaluator, or control interface still refers to the same decision-relevant
> object under intervention, context shift, or system change?
