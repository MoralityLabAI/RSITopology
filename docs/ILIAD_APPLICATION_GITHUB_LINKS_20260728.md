# Iliad Fellowship: GitHub research links

Patrick Dugan / Morality Lab AI  
Prepared 2026-07-28

This is the short application-facing index for two connected research
programs:

- the AI Safety Millennium Problems (ASMP), a collection of versioned
  mathematical questions, bounded theorem seeds, exact experiments, and
  explicit resolution obligations; and
- identity attestation for mechanistic interpretability, using lineage and
  holonomy to test whether a monitor, feature, or edit direction continues to
  denote the same object across contexts, checkpoints, and runtime
  precisions.

### Holonomy and identity attestation

5. [The missing phase of bundle lineage](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/RSITopology/blob/1556649a8d35fd51550f6d9c02a301d016879fa7/reports/monitor_holonomy_insight.md)  
   The core synthetic construction: two systems have nearly identical local
   lineage, while one accumulates a measured 62.173-degree loop rotation.
   This isolates why pairwise subspace similarity cannot certify a globally
   consistent signed coordinate.

6. [Qwen0.8B precision-geometry paper](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/RSITopology/blob/1556649a8d35fd51550f6d9c02a301d016879fa7/reports/QWEN08_PRECISION_GEOMETRY_PAPER_V0_1.md)  
   The real-model boundary result: graded geometry degrades under
   quantization, but the binary topological outcome is recognized as
   non-live. The paper turns that failure into a general liveness certificate
   for rank-one holonomy claims.

## Suggested application description

> I develop falsifiable mathematical instruments for AI control and scalable
> oversight. My ASMP program turns long-horizon safety questions into precise
> mathematical statements, prospective tests, and explicit resolution
> obligations. My holonomy work tests whether mechanistic features and
> monitors retain a coherent identity across contexts, checkpoints, and
> precision changes. The linked Qwen study illustrates the governing
> discipline: it found measurable geometric degradation under quantization,
> but also proved that the apparent topological stability was forced and
> therefore could not count as evidence.

## Claim boundary

These links document bounded mathematical results, exact finite experiments,
synthetic instrument calibrations, and early real-model falsification studies.
They do not claim that any ASMP is resolved at full scope, that transformer
capabilities are topological, or that recursive self-improvement has been
demonstrated.
