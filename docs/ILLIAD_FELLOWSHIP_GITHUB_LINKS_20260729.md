## Paste-ready application description

> I am developing a mathematical and experimental identity layer for
> mechanistic interpretability: tools that test whether a feature or monitor
> continues to denote the same object across contexts, checkpoints, and
> deployment precisions. The work combines gauge-invariant subspace transport,
> loop closure, preregistered falsification, and executable receipts. In
> parallel, I maintain a versioned AI Safety Millennium Problems agenda that
> turns broad safety questions into explicit mathematical obligations and
> records negative results without relaxing gates after observation. The
> linked repository includes synthetic theorems, exact finite censuses,
> real-model Qwen studies, hostile audits, and an interactive receipt viewer.

## Claim boundary

The holonomy work establishes synthetic separation results and bounded
real-model measurements; it does not establish that transformer capabilities
are topological. The ASMP documents are proposed research problems and
internally reviewed candidate results, not established prize problems or
externally accepted resolutions.
