# Iliad Fellowship: selected research links

Patrick Dugan / Morality Lab AI  
Prepared 2026-07-29

## Short description

I develop falsifiable mathematical instruments for AI control and scalable
oversight. The work has two connected branches:

1. the AI Safety Millennium Problems (ASMP) program, which turns broad safety
   questions into versioned mathematical statements, exact bounded results,
   prospective experiments, and explicit resolution obligations; and
2. an identity-attestation program for mechanistic interpretability, which
   tests whether a monitor, feature, or edit direction continues to denote the
   same object across contexts, checkpoints, and runtime precisions.

The methodological rule joining them is simple: an instrument must be capable
of failing. Negative, unavailable, and non-live outcomes remain visible rather
than being promoted into evidence.

### ASMP program

1. [Canonical ASMP problem statements](../ultra-experiments/millennium/AI_SAFETY_MILLENNIUM_PROBLEMS_v0_1.md)  
   Formal objects, quantifier order, liveness requirements, computability
   boundaries, and two-sided resolution standards for the original seven
   candidate problems.

2. [ASMP peer-review summary and progress map](../ultra-experiments/millennium/PROBLEM_PROGRESS_SUMMARY_v0_1.md)  
   The concise evidence map and conservative status ledger.

3. [Weak-verifier moment ladder](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp3_weak_verifier_frontier/MOMENT_ORDER_RESULT_v0_1_3.md)  
   Exact primal and dual certificates show that, for a frozen nine-judge
   panel, marginal accuracy and pairwise diversity can certify failure but
   cannot certify success. Fourth-order dependence information is the first
   level sufficient to certify the registered 5% majority-error target.

4. [ASMP-9 reward-gauge resolution audit](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/RESOLUTION_AUDIT_v0_33_DEVELOPMENT.md)  
   A transparent audit of a long theorem-and-instrument chain for value
   identification modulo reward gauge. It separates proved finite results,
   unregistered development work, and the remaining load-bearing obligations
   rather than presenting a partial chain as a resolution.

### Holonomy and mechanistic identity

5. [The missing phase of bundle lineage](../reports/monitor_holonomy_insight.md)  
   A matched synthetic construction keeps local subspace lineage near 0.99 in
   both conditions while one closed loop returns a signed coordinate rotated
   by 62.173 degrees. It isolates why pairwise feature similarity cannot
   certify globally consistent signed identity.

6. [Qwen0.8B precision-geometry and holonomy paper](../reports/QWEN08_PRECISION_GEOMETRY_PAPER_V0_1.md)  
   The real-model boundary test: quantization compresses support margins and
   erodes the rank-one frustration margin, while a liveness theorem explains
   why the observed binary topology was unable to change on the measured
   graph.

## Application-ready description

> I develop falsifiable mathematical instruments for AI control and scalable
> oversight. My ASMP program formalizes long-horizon safety questions with
> explicit quantifiers, liveness conditions, resolution obligations, and
> prospectively registered tests. My holonomy work asks whether mechanistic
> features and monitors retain a coherent identity across contexts,
> checkpoints, and precision changes. The linked Qwen study is representative
> of my approach: it found real geometric degradation under quantization, but
> also proved that the apparent topological stability was forced and therefore
> was not evidence. I want to turn this combination of mathematical structure,
> adversarial self-critique, and real-model falsification into practical
> control and oversight tools.

## Claim boundary

These artifacts are a research agenda, bounded mathematical results, exact
finite experiments, synthetic instrument calibrations, and early real-model
falsification studies. They do not resolve an ASMP at its full stated scope,
establish that transformer capabilities are topological, or demonstrate
recursive self-improvement.
