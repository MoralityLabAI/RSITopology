Patrick Dugan / Morality Lab AI

## Short research description

I develop falsifiable mathematical instruments for AI control and scalable
oversight. The work has two connected branches:

1. the **AI Safety Millennium Problems (ASMP)** program, which turns broad
   safety questions into versioned mathematical statements, exact finite
   experiments, prospective gates, and explicit claim boundaries; and
2. an **identity-attestation** program for mechanistic interpretability, which
   tests whether a monitor, feature, or edit direction continues to denote the
   same object across contexts, checkpoints, and runtime precisions.

The common methodological rule is that an instrument must be capable of
failing. Negative and unavailable outcomes remain visible; bounded results are
not promoted into general theorems; and synthetic controls are not presented
as transformer evidence.

  A constructive theorem gives the exact coefficient width needed to separate
  bounded integer reward rays under strictly positive threshold ambiguity. The
  record also preserves the failed first statement whose inclusion of the

- [ASMP-9: exact-tie comparison-width theorem](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/zero_radius_theorem_v0_4/PUBLIC_SUMMARY_v0_4.md)

- [ASMP-9: finite-sample preference-access theorem](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/finite_sample_v0_5/PUBLIC_SUMMARY_v0_5_1.md)
  A known noisy sign-and-tie channel preserves the sharp width as a liveness
  boundary and exposes a quadratic nonadaptive versus logarithmic-depth
  adaptive access separation. Discounted and behavioral identification remain
  open.

- [ASMP-9: discounted-shaping quotient theorem](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/discounted_shaping_v0_6/PUBLIC_SUMMARY_v0_6.md)
  Discounted potential shaping changes ordinary cycle cohomology into a
  gain-graph quotient. The result gives its exact balanced-component rank and
  the boundary signatures required for finite trajectory comparisons to
  remain shaping-invariant; behavioral identification remains open.

- [ASMP-9: behavioral reconstruction and model-checking split](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/behavioral_wellposedness_v0_7/PUBLIC_SUMMARY_v0_7.md)
  A spanning forest reconstructs scalar preferences only under a coherence
  promise; cycle access is the additional test that the scalar exists. A
  matched deterministic-policy arm gives sharp adaptive and nonadaptive
  ambiguity radii and proves finite exact identification impossible for a
  continuous reward coordinate.

- [ASMP-9: unknown response-link obstruction](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/unknown_link_v0_8/PUBLIC_SUMMARY_v0_8.md)
  A known injective response link with unknown inverse temperature identifies
  utility up to positive scale, while an unrestricted unknown monotone link
  admits exact non-affine three-item observational equivalences. The result is
  a finite-design population obstruction, not a general IRL theorem.

- [ASMP-9: finite-sample coherence certificate](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/finite_sample_coherence_v0_9/PUBLIC_SUMMARY_v0_9.md)
  A simultaneous cycle test with coherent, incoherent, inconclusive, and
  unavailable outcomes, plus a conservative graph-dependent sample bound.
  It prevents finite-sample non-rejection from being reported as proof that a
  scalar value model exists.

- [ASMP-9: exact finite-MDP policy-access geometry](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp9_reward_gauge_census/finite_mdp_access_v0_10/PUBLIC_SUMMARY_v0_10.md)

  on whether full stochastic probabilities or only action identities are

- [ASMP-11: covering-mediated access frontier](../ultra-experiments/millennium/asmp11_access_ladder/covering_frontier_v0_2/RESULT_v0_2.md)

## Holonomy and identity attestation

### Mathematical construction

- [The missing phase of bundle lineage](../reports/monitor_holonomy_insight.md)  
  A matched synthetic control holds local lineage near `0.99` in both arms
  while one loop returns a signed coordinate unchanged and the other returns
  it rotated by `62.173` degrees. This establishes why pairwise subspace
  similarity alone cannot certify global signed identity.

- [Interactive Gödel-globe workbench](../godel-globe/index.html) and [design memo](GODEL_GLOBES_V0_1.md)  
  A no-build browser visualization for edge lineage, loop receipts,
  orientation reversals, certification levels, and measured parallel
  transport. The globe is presentational; the rendered angles come only from
  receipts.

### Real-model falsification and boundary finding

- [Qwen0.8B precision-geometry paper](../reports/QWEN08_PRECISION_GEOMETRY_PAPER_V0_1.md)  
  Paired NF4/fp16-compute versus fp16-weight measurements. The study finds a
  graded geometric degradation but proves that the observed all-positive
  rank-one holonomy was forced by the cycle-angle budget. The resulting
  liveness theorem says when a binary topological test was capable of failing.

- [Qwen0.8B dense-local holonomy result](../reports/QWEN08_DENSE_LOCAL_HOLONOMY_V0_1.md)  
  Sixteen replicas recovered a cross-checkpoint rank-one lineage object, but
  context edges prevented a higher common rank and the causal stage remained
  closed. The result localizes the limiting axis instead of treating a stopped
  gate as evidence of flatness.

- [Earlier Qwen falsification report](../reports/QWEN08_HOLONOMY_FALSIFICATION_V0_1.md)  
  Records the failure of the initial local object and the distinction between a
  pooled descriptive superstructure and a certifiable local bundle.

## Suggested application wording

> I am developing a research program around falsifiable mathematical
> instruments for AI control and scalable oversight. My ASMP work formalizes
> long-horizon safety questions with prospective claim boundaries and exact
> finite experiments; my holonomy work asks whether mechanistic features and
> monitors retain a coherent identity across contexts, checkpoints, and
> precision changes. The linked Qwen study is representative of my approach:
> it found real geometric degradation under quantization, but also proved that
> the apparent topological stability was forced and therefore not evidence.
> I want to develop this combination of mathematical structure, adversarial
> self-critique, and real-model falsification into usable control and oversight
> tools.

## Claim boundary

These artifacts constitute a research agenda, bounded mathematical results,
synthetic instrument calibrations, and early real-model falsification studies.
They do not resolve any ASMP at its full stated scope, establish that
transformer capability is topological, or demonstrate recursive
self-improvement. Their contribution is to make those larger claims more
precise, more testable, and harder to overstate.
