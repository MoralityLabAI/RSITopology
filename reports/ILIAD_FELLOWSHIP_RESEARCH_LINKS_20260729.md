## Holonomy and identity attestation

- [The missing phase of bundle lineage](monitor_holonomy_insight.md):
  the central synthetic construction. Two systems have matched local lineage,
  yet one accumulates a measured 62.17-degree loop rotation and loses signed
  coordinate identity.
- [Identity measurements as a signed-control risk bound](../docs/CONTROL_RISK_THEOREM.md):
  a proved bound combining edge-wise lineage loss, loop holonomy, and
  orientation reversal into a conservative signed-control authorization rule.
- [Holonomy causal path-transfer falsification](holonomy_causal_transfer_v0_1.md):
  a preregistered synthetic test showing when holonomy adds causal predictive
  information beyond local lineage, including matched and gauge-invariance
  controls.
- [Qwen0.8B precision-geometry paper](QWEN08_PRECISION_GEOMETRY_PAPER_V0_1.md):
  the main real-model result. Four-bit quantization compresses support margins
  and erodes the frustration margin, while the binary rank-one holonomy class
  is shown to be geometrically forced and therefore non-informative on this
  capture.
- [Edit-sectioning calibration](../docs/EDIT_SECTIONING_V03_RESULTS.md):
  a patchwise edit-planning instrument that wins when holonomy patches match
  edit identity and loses, as analytically predicted, when pooling bias
  dominates.
- [Identity-attestation integration](../docs/IDENTITY_ATTESTATION_INTEGRATION.md):
  the implemented anchor registry and certification API for the control
  harness, Blue Beam tamper tests, and VPD edit program.
- [Gödel-globe visualization](../godel-globe/README.md):
  a no-build interactive viewer for lineage, loop holonomy, determinant flags,
  certification levels, and receipt provenance.

## Suggested application description

I am developing a mathematical identity-attestation layer for AI control:
tools that test whether a learned monitor, reward feature, or edit coordinate
continues to denote the same object across contexts and model states. The work
combines spectral bundles, gauge-invariant lineage and holonomy, conservative
control bounds, adversarial matched controls, and hash-bound experimental
receipts. Alongside it, I maintain a versioned set of AI Safety Millennium
Problem candidates and use prospectively registered finite theorem seeds and
falsification experiments to turn broad safety questions into auditable
mathematical obligations. The program reports negative results as outcomes:
all seven original ASMPs have bounded work, none is claimed fully resolved, and
the first real-model holonomy study explicitly proves when its topological
diagnostic was incapable of changing.

## Claim boundary

The ASMP documents are a proposed research agenda, not recognized prize
problems. Bounded theorem seeds and experiments do not constitute full
resolutions. The holonomy controls establish mathematical and measurement
properties; the Qwen0.8B paper is the principal real-model result and does not
claim recursive self-improvement, general monitor sufficiency, or successful
weight editing.
