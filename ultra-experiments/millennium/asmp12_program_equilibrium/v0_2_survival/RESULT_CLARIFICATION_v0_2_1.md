# ASMP-12 v0.2 result clarification

This additive note resolves three possible ambiguities in the canonical
`RESULT_v0_2.md`. It changes no registered gate, raw result, or receipt.

1. **Death partition.** The 174 cooperative-death catalogs partition into 134
   duplicate-extensional catalogs and 40 non-extensional catalogs. Within the
   latter 40, 16 use the registered syntax-equivalence exploit and 24 use other
   non-extensional mechanisms. Thus `134+16+24=174`.
2. **Margin complement.** Of 32,064 checked cooperative-equilibrium margins,
   12,384 are strictly positive and 19,680 are exactly zero. No positive margin
   was rejected by an additional threshold. Budget-one profiles without a
   nontrivial alternative are outside this denominator.
3. **Gates versus tests.** Seven preregistered scientific gates were evaluated
   by the v0.2 runner. The “eight tests” count consists of four v0.2 software
   tests and four v0.1 regression tests. These are different validation layers,
   not inconsistent counts.

The `T<=R` zero applies to cooperative-equilibrium deaths. Payoff-vector deaths
elsewhere in the equilibrium correspondence occur in lower-temptation cells
and remain reported.
