# They Sing bounded-source-conditioning hypothesis amendment v0.1

## Status

Pre-run conceptual amendment. It replaces the threshold-only curve proposed in
the original ASMP-12 frame-up. It is not a complete They Sing registration;
model, prompts, scenarios, budgets, seeds, decoding, and statistical margins
must still be frozen in the consumer repository before outcomes exist.

## Outcome classes

For otherwise matched agents receiving opponent-policy text under increasing
verification-token budgets:

1. **Flat response:** cooperation and policy-sensitive reasoning do not change
   beyond the registered noise margin. Interpretation: the substrate has not
   demonstrated verification-mediated source conditioning.
2. **Threshold-like response:** cooperation or calibrated policy discrimination
   appears and then remains stable. Interpretation: evidence for a coarse
   verification threshold in the tested range.
3. **Birth/death response:** cooperation appears at one budget and falls at a
   larger budget while policy-sensitive reasoning and task competence remain
   live. Interpretation: candidate behavioral signature of source conditioning
   that discovers a newly profitable deviation as verification resources grow.

The third outcome is not presumed. It is the distinctive alternative the
finite census makes worth testing.

## Controls needed to call a death source-conditioned

- hide or shuffle opponent policies while holding token budget fixed;
- provide matched non-policy text of equal length;
- verify that larger budgets actually improve registered policy-comprehension
  questions;
- reproduce the adjacent-budget decline across independent scenarios/seeds;
- separate payoff-driven defection from context-window truncation or generic
  degradation; and
- require structured reasoning evidence that cites the newly available
  opponent-policy distinction without exposing private chain of thought.

A noisy nonmonotone sample path is not a death event. The registration needs a
simultaneous interval or hierarchical contrast whose adverse bound clears a
frozen practical margin.

## Claim boundary

Even a validated birth/death curve would show only that the tested language
models condition behavior on supplied policy text under the frozen game. It
would not establish proof search, bounded Löbian reasoning, program equilibrium
in the formal sense, or scalable cooperation among deployed systems.
