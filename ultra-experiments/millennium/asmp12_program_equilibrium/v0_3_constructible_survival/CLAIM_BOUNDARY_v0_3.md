# ASMP-12 constructible survival claim boundary v0.3

## Task result supported by a passing report

A passing report supports an explicit finite constructible correspondence for
the registered source-table catalog and rational budget-by-temptation grid:

- a complete exact weighted profitable-deviation graph at every cell;
- verified gain-preserving inclusions along the nested budget axis;
- underlying-graph adjacent-union zigzags along the temptation axis; and
- a separate derived process of cooperative sink births, deaths, and signed
  margin certificates.

This realizes the graph-level object requested after v0.2. The construction is
finite and combinatorial; it is not presented as a new category-theoretic or
game-theoretic theorem.

## Reliability support

Reliability comes from exact rational arithmetic, complete finite enumeration,
typed-map checks, independent reconstruction, exact event witnesses, and five
robustness probes. The graph digest root identifies the deterministic cell
collection summarized by a report. Reliability applies only to the frozen
catalog, payoff families, grid, and strict-edge semantics.

As of v0.3.1, "registered" means exact equality to the machine manifest: the
ordered 512-catalog sequence, parameter values, complete Cartesian graph keys,
and every ordered budget and temptation adjacency. Counts alone are not
registry evidence. Exact relations, decorations, margins, and gain witnesses
must match an import-independent reconstruction, and the released compact
bundle must bind its source checkpoint and all evidence roots.

## Claims not supported

The equilibrium sets are not called an ordinary bifiltration. This phase does
not support:

- an ordinary two-parameter persistence module of equilibrium sets;
- weight-preserving maps across changing temptation values;
- homology, barcode, bottleneck, interleaving, or general stability claims;
- mixed, correlated, or unrestricted program-equilibrium characterizations;
- an equilibrium-selection or learning dynamic; or
- a behavioral bridge to language models or deployed agents.

The adjacent unions are explicit graph correspondences. Any later algebraic
functor must state its category, maps, coefficient system, and stability target
before deriving a persistence claim.

## Operational boundary

The report is deterministic CPU work with no GPU, network, or repository
writes. It does not alter v0.2, authorize new training, or update shared ASMP
status documents.
