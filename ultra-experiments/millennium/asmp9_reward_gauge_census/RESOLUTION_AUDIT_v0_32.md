# ASMP-9 resolution audit after v0.32

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.32 supplies the behavioral source missing from v0.30-v0.31, but
only under a declared mixture-affine access grammar:

```text
common global standard-gamble ruler
  -> normalized scalar rectangle;

redundant compound lotteries
  -> finite mixture-affinity falsification;

rectangle cross-differences
  -> additive semantic certificate;

shared cell intervals
  -> correlated v0.31 uncertainty handoff.
```

Deterministic ordinal rankings and row-local standard-gamble rulers each
admit exact additive-versus-interacting indistinguishability witnesses.

## Status against the five obligations

### 1. Maximal invariance groups

**Partially addressed in declared finite models; open generally.**

Under a mixture-affine representation with common anchors, the v0.32 scalar
table is normalized to a common positive-affine gauge. Zero rectangle
cross-difference is invariant under the residual global affine freedom.

The theorem does not show that mixture affinity is the correct or maximal
behavioral equivalence for humans, models, strategic demonstrators,
history-dependent rewards, or non-expected-utility preferences.

### 2. Necessary and sufficient access conditions

**Sharp for the registered finite binary standard-gamble grammar; open
generally.**

Version v0.32 gives:

- exact cell acquisition from a common global ruler;
- a necessary-and-sufficient cross-difference criterion for finite rectangle
  additivity;
- a complete-cell necessity witness;
- an ordinal-only impossibility witness; and
- a row-local-scale gluing impossibility witness.

The global-anchor and objective-lottery interface is strong. The finite
compound-lottery audit can falsify mixture affinity on registered cells but
cannot prove the axioms on the full lottery space.

### 3. Sharp query, sample, and intervention-order bounds

**The population grid bound is sharp; the finite-sample and downstream
complexity remain incomplete.**

For `N` unrestricted centered-dyadic cells at depth `d`, v0.32 gives the
matching `Nd` binary-query upper and transcript lower bounds.

For the registered independent Bernoulli response floor, the exact binomial
calculation makes 43 the smallest odd repeat count satisfying the frozen
union-bound target. This is not a minimax theorem. It does not jointly
optimize cell allocation, mixture-audit allocation, occupancy estimation,
and policy regret under dependent observations.

### 4. Robustness to behavioral misspecification

**Finite registered-cell falsification and deterministic interval propagation
are addressed; broad misspecification is open.**

The compound-lottery controls distinguish a zero affine residual from a
planted `1/16` distortion and preserve an inconclusive state under
overlapping widths. The standard-gamble certainty-equivalent cells propagate
through the rectangle incidence without discarding shared-cell cancellation.

The result assumes common anchors, a common response midpoint, independent
repeats, and an admitted correct-sign floor. Rank-dependent utility,
prospect-theoretic probability weighting, context-varying anchors, strategic
answers, and dependent responses remain outside the theorem.

### 5. No-go theorem without a coherent latent value object

**Several exact witnesses exist; a general classification remains open.**

The current chain now includes:

- non-affine utility ambiguity under unknown monotone links;
- context-nuisance confounding;
- mechanics-only semantic nonidentification;
- gain-one mechanical channel erasure;
- deterministic-order equivalence between additive and interacting tables;
  and
- row-local-ruler equivalence between globally additive and interacting
  tables.

These establish failure for named access classes. They do not characterize
all demonstrator laws that lack a coherent scalar quotient.

## Remaining load-bearing sequence

### A. Sharp joint stochastic policy complexity

Combine behavioral cell acquisition, mixture-affinity audits, context
projection, occupancy estimation, graph and quotient conditioning, shared
semantic cells, and policy margins in one adaptive minimax theorem.

### B. Coarser environment equivalence

Replace exact registered occupancy rows with a declared MDP homomorphism,
bisimulation, simulation metric, or occupancy-equivalence class. Determine
when semantic interventions preserve the downstream query under changed raw
mechanics.

### C. General misspecification and no-go classification

Characterize which non-expected-utility, context-dependent,
history-dependent, or strategic demonstrator laws admit any coherent
quotient-valued target, and give matching indistinguishability results when
they do not.

## Epistemic boundary

Version v0.32 shows that one strong behavioral interface can produce and test
the scalar rectangle assumed by v0.30-v0.31. It does not show that humans or
models satisfy that interface, that a real semantic unit is stable, or that
ASMP-9 is resolved.
