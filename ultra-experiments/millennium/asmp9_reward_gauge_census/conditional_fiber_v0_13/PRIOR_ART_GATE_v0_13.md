# ASMP-9 v0.13 prior-art gate

## Decision

Treat the conditional-fiber theorem as a consolidation of established
exponential-family and algebraic-statistics results. Do not claim new
conditional-inference, toric-model, Markov-basis, or minimax-testing theory.

The load-bearing prior art is:

- Diaconis and Sturmfels, *Algebraic Algorithms for Sampling from Conditional
  Distributions* (1998), which establishes the Markov-basis framework for
  exact conditional inference in discrete exponential families:
  <https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1030563990>.
- Sturmfels and Welker, *Commutative Algebra of Statistical Ranking* (2012),
  which characterizes the Bradley-Terry model as a toric model generated by
  graph circuits:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.1597>.
- Jiang, Lim, Yao, and Ye, *Statistical Ranking and Combinatorial Hodge
  Theory* (2011), which separates gradient and cyclic ranking flows:
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-010-0419-x>.
- Makur and Singh, *Minimax Hypothesis Testing for the Bradley-Terry-Luce
  Model* (2024), which supplies a materially broader minimax goodness-of-fit
  theory:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08360>.
- Skalse and Abate, *Partial Identifiability and Misspecification in Inverse
  Reinforcement Learning* (2024), which already gives a broad framework for
  reward ambiguity and behavioral-model misspecification:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15951>.

## Surviving ASMP-9 role

The useful contribution is an exact access ledger specialized to the existing
ASMP-9 graph grammar:

1. show which sufficient statistic removes the scalar-value nuisance;
2. identify the exact quotient visible in the resulting conditional law;
3. make fiber rank a mandatory finite-sample liveness condition;
4. give a closed-form one-cycle conditional family and exact test; and
5. compare its exact finite thresholds with the conservative v0.9
   simultaneous-band instrument without claiming that the two procedures
   answer identical questions.

The conditional test can reject scalar coherence. Non-rejection is not proof
that a scalar value exists. Conditioning also changes unconditional power and
availability, which must be reported rather than hidden.
