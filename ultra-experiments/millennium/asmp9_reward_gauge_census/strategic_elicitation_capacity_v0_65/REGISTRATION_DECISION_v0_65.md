# ASMP-9 v0.65 registration decision

## Decision

```text
do_not_register
```

Version v0.65 remains unregistered theorem development and is not
claim-eligible.

## Reason

Prospective registration is valuable when a run can resolve a live
scientific uncertainty.  That condition is not met here:

1. the deterministic complete-strategy reduction is a finite specialization
   of classical revelation and menu principles;
2. the random-problem-selection escape, including the full-ranking/random-pair
   mechanism, is explicit in Azrieli, Chambers, and Healy (2021);
3. the full-support and adjacent-swap calculations are elementary finite
   consequences of that access model; and
4. another sealed replay of these known cells would not test whether the
   assumptions hold for a human, model, or dynamically changing target.

The development package is retained because it closes a false route: one must
not infer that deterministic-menu impossibility survives the addition of
post-commitment random consequences.  Conversely, one must not cite the
classical positive construction without pricing its expected-utility,
objective-randomization, and commitment assumptions.

## Authorized use

The package may:

- update the ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix;
- define the static strategic access boundary;
- supply executable finite checks and counterexamples; and
- motivate a prospective physical or dynamic bridge.

It may not be presented as:

- a novel mechanism-design theorem;
- a prospectively verified ASMP result;
- evidence that model or human values are identifiable; or
- a resolution of ASMP-9.

## Next registration-worthy target

A successor should test an uncertainty not answered by the classical
construction.  Two admissible targets are:

1. **dynamic strategic response:** the latent target or utility changes after
   observing earlier sampled consequences, with a frozen equivalence loss and
   commitment chronology; or
2. **physical access validation:** a human or model bridge testing whether
   pre-draw commitment, objective randomization, pairwise consequence
   sensitivity, response stability, and expected-utility consistency hold
   strongly enough for the theoretical margin to be operational.

Any successor must state which histories are visible, when the target may
change, which consequences are actually enforced, and what decision loss
defines successful identification.
