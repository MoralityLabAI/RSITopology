## Attribution boundary

- Risk-set and randomization comparison is classical Blackwell theory.
- Additive deficiency and restriction to decision-problem types is classical
  Le Cam/Torgersen theory.
- The equivalence between injective deterministic test signatures and Test
  Cover is definitional; NP-hardness is classical.
- The candidate contribution is the ASMP-9-specific risk-polytope access
  compiler and the exact comparison of minimal access antichains under
  multiple reward/policy loss types.

No absence-of-prior-art claim is made.
