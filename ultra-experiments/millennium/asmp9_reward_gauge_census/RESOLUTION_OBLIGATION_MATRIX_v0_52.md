# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.52

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.52 closes one finite structural seam: optimizing over all evidence
orders is complete among every valid deterministic direct upper confidence
map. The theorem is a classical Buehler self-ordering corollary, verified
exactly over a complete multilevel finite universe.

It does not close aggregate randomized coverage, continuous or strategic
uncertainty, the physical preference-access problem, or the
scalar-versus-relation-versus-no-object classification.

## What v0.52 establishes

For any finite valid deterministic direct map `u`, sort outcomes by
nondecreasing `u` and refine ties arbitrarily. The induced Buehler map `U_pi`
satisfies

```text
U_pi <= u
```

pointwise. Therefore every coordinatewise nondecreasing objective has the same
minimum over all valid deterministic maps as over all Buehler outcome orders.

The prospective finite check covered:

```text
148 tables
3,996 candidate direct maps
1,494 valid direct maps
3,342 tie-refined dominance checks
1,480 global optimum comparisons
0 mismatches.
```

## What v0.52 does not establish

- Optimality under aggregate randomized coverage.
- Two-sided confidence-set completeness.
- Continuous or statistic-independent asymptotic completeness.
- Robustness to correlated, adaptive, or strategic misspecification.
- A calibrated physical preference channel.
- Existence or uniqueness of a coherent scalar latent value.
- A full ASMP-9 resolution.

## Next load-bearing target

Let a randomized direct procedure choose a report distribution conditional on
the observed outcome. Coverage is required only after averaging over external
randomization:

```text
P_theta(U(X,Z) >= d(theta)) >= 1-alpha.
```

Unlike v0.51, the deterministic components of this procedure need not be
individually valid. The next target is:

1. formulate the exact finite primal/dual LP;
2. prove whether every optimum has an interpretable sparse extreme point;
3. find the smallest strict improvement over deterministic direct maps, or
   prove equality in the declared grammar; and
4. distinguish aggregate validity from operational desirability.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.52 is a directly subsumed Buehler-optimality specialization. It
closes a finite deterministic instrument, not general randomized confidence
inference. It does not identify real values, validate human preference access,
prove continuous or strategic robustness, or resolve ASMP-9.
