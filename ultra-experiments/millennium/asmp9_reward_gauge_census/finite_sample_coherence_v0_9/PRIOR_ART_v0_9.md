# ASMP-9 v0.9 prior-art boundary

## Primary anchors

1. Bradley and Terry, *Rank analysis of incomplete block designs: I. The
   method of paired comparisons*, Biometrika 39 (1952), 324-345,
   DOI `10.1093/biomet/39.3-4.324`.

   The logistic paired-comparison model is classical.

2. Clopper and Pearson, *The use of confidence or fiducial limits illustrated
   in the case of the binomial*, Biometrika 26 (1934), 404-413,
   DOI `10.1093/biomet/26.4.404`.

   Exact binomial intervals long predate this experiment. The current theorem
   uses a simpler Hoeffding/union-bound construction because its simultaneous
   validity and graph-dependent propagation are transparent.

3. Jiang, Lim, Yao, and Ye, *Statistical ranking and combinatorial Hodge
   theory*, Mathematical Programming 127 (2011), 203-244,
   DOI `10.1007/s10107-010-0419-x`.

   Pairwise data as edge flows, gradient ranking, and cyclic inconsistency are
   established HodgeRank ideas. ASMP-9 v0.9 does not claim them as new.

## Exact contribution boundary

The intended contribution is an auditable specialization:

- a prospectively frozen simultaneous edge event;
- explicit propagation to a deterministic fundamental-cycle basis;
- a total `coherent / incoherent / inconclusive / unavailable` decision;
- a graph-dependent sufficient sample bound; and
- gates that prevent non-rejection from being reported as scalar coherence.

This is an instrument and finite theorem specialization, not a new
paired-comparison or concentration theory.
