# ASMP-9 v0.17 prior-art gate

## Decision

Do not claim novelty for residual networks, circulation decomposition,
network-flow polytope dimensions, strong orientations, coherent-system
minimal cut sets, reliability polynomials, or reliability allocation.

The following are direct anchors:

- Ahuja, Magnanti, and Orlin, *Network Flows: Theory, Algorithms, and
  Applications* (1993), for residual networks and path/cycle flow
  decomposition:
  <https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/Ahuja-Network-Flows-Theory-Algorithms-and-Applications/P200000003456/9780136175490>.
- Schrijver, *Combinatorial Optimization: Polyhedra and Efficiency* (2003),
  for flow spaces and polyhedral combinatorics:
  <https://link.springer.com/book/9783540443896>.
- Robbins (1939), for the equivalence between bridgeless undirected graphs
  and strongly orientable graphs; a modern statement and extension is
  discussed by Schlipf and Schmidt:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.07389>.
- Colbourn's network-reliability line and modern reliability texts, for
  reliability polynomials, path sets, and minimal cut sets:
  <https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/net.3230180102> and
  <https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9781119224006>.
- El-Neweihi, Proschan, and Sethuraman (1986), for reliability allocation,
  majorization, and balanced series/parallel systems:
  <https://doi.org/10.2307/3214014>.
- Georgiadis, Italiano, and Parotsidis (2020), for strong connectivity under
  directed edge and vertex failures:
  <https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1258530>.

## Likely classical ingredients

The residual-rank proof is a specialization of standard network-flow
polytope and circulation-space facts. The minimal-bad-support exponent is a
finite coherent-system/minimal-cut large-deviation argument followed by a
zero-sum linear program. Neither should be advertised as a new general
network-reliability theorem without a deeper literature audit.

## Surviving ASMP-9 contribution

The candidate contribution is the exact composition of those ingredients
for the conditional reward-gauge access ledger:

1. full conditional-fiber quotient rank becomes residual-cycle liveness;
2. binomial counts compress to a ternary edge reliability model;
3. coordinatewise concavity makes endpoint reduction exact;
4. the minimal bad supports give the access-allocation exponent; and
5. the five-edge theta graph refutes uniform allocation both finitely and
   asymptotically.

This closes a stated transfer question from v0.16 and supplies the graph
object needed for a prospectively registered successor. It is not a novelty
claim for flow or reliability theory and not an ASMP-9 resolution.
