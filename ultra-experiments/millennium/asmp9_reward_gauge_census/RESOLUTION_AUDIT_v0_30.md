# ASMP-9 resolution audit after v0.30

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.30 closes the exact identity-level realization question behind the
v0.28 calibrated offset and proves a matching semantic limit:

```text
identical MDP mechanics
  -> base policy laws and occupancies preserved;

complete calibrated scalar rectangle
  -> declared consequence is additive;

mechanics alone
  -> semantic coefficient remains unidentifiable.
```

The result converts “known numeraire” from one unexamined assumption into two
separate certificates. It also gives a sharp `2 epsilon` propagation rule for
approximate semantic calibration.

## Status against the five obligations

### 1. Maximal invariance groups

**Partially addressed in declared finite linear models; open generally.**

The program distinguishes shaping, positive scale, response-link shape,
midpoint drift, component gauge, observation kernel, decision-null gauge, and
now the semantic consequence coefficient.

Version v0.30 proves that mechanics do not identify that coefficient and that
unknown positive scaling remains coupled under the registered response-link
class. The maximal invariance group for history-dependent rewards,
nonlinear representations, strategic demonstrators, and non-expected-utility
preferences remains open.

### 2. Necessary and sufficient access conditions

**Sharp under the registered exact mechanics and scalar-table access; open
under behavioral and coarser environment access.**

Version v0.28 gives the calibrated occupancy criterion `ker(X)=G`. Version
v0.29 determines when `G` is decision-null. Version v0.30 supplies:

- a sufficient exact mechanics certificate preserving the original occupancy
  rows;
- a necessary-and-sufficient anchored scalar-table test for declared additive
  calibration;
- total abstention on incomplete semantic coverage; and
- a combined eligibility rule requiring both certificates.

The exact mechanics rule is not a maximal MDP-equivalence theorem. More
importantly, the scalar table is assumed available rather than inferred from
ordinal or noisy behavioral responses.

### 3. Sharp query, sample, and intervention-order bounds

**Several population and finite-table bounds are sharp; stochastic policy and
semantic-calibration complexity remain incomplete.**

The calibrated rectangle has exactly `m(n-1)` independent constraints, and
every omission admits a witness. The registered five-by-six control verified
rank 25 and all 25 omission witnesses.

Earlier versions supply scalar bisection, finite-sample link-margin,
graph-allocation, quotient-conditioning, and finite-policy regret constants.
The sample complexity of constructing the scalar consequence table from
behavioral observations remains open.

### 4. Robustness to behavioral misspecification

**One approximate semantic factor is now sharp; the joint robustness theorem
remains open.**

If each anchored semantic residual is bounded by `epsilon`, the two-sided
offset error is bounded by `2 epsilon`, sharply. This factor can be propagated
into v0.28 localization and v0.29 regret.

Still missing is one theorem combining mechanical transition/feature drift,
midpoint drift, link-margin uncertainty, dependent responses, occupancy
estimation, quotient conditioning, and policy regret.

### 5. No-go theorem without a coherent latent value object

**The no-go branch is stronger but incomplete.**

The same non-reward MDP mechanics supports calibrated, unknown-scale, and
context-interacting semantic tables. No mechanics-only checker can distinguish
them. Complete population access also leaves an unknown consequence
coefficient scale-coupled under the rescalable-link class.

This proves that a product-MDP construction cannot create a semantic unit. It
does not classify every inconsistent or non-scalar demonstrator law.

## Next load-bearing sequence

### A. Joint approximate factorization

Combine bounded mechanical drift, semantic calibration residual, context
midpoint drift, and occupancy-localization error into one set-valued quotient
certificate. Propagate the resulting support function through the v0.29
policy decision.

### B. Behavioral acquisition of the semantic rectangle

Freeze a response model or additive-conjoint access grammar under which the
scalar consequence increments can be estimated or falsified. Derive matching
indistinguishability results when the qualitative measurement axioms fail.

### C. Sharp stochastic policy complexity

Combine response margins, graph and quotient spectral floors, adaptive
allocation, occupancy estimation, and policy margins in one minimax theorem.

### D. Coarser environment equivalence

Replace byte-identical mechanics with a declared MDP homomorphism,
bisimulation, or occupancy-equivalence class. Characterize when consequence
interventions preserve the exact downstream query despite changing the raw
MDP description.

## Epistemic boundary

Version v0.30 proves what an exact conservative consequence certificate must
contain and why mechanics cannot supply its semantic unit. It does not show
that the semantic evidence exists for humans or models, or that ASMP-9 is
resolved.
