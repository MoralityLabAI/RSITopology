# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.63 verification

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.63 prospectively verifies the first exact full-dimensional
cross-tier corruption moduli in the suite.  It closes the v0.61 matrix's
small-class modulus action without validating any behavioral premise or
supplying the missing hidden-menu, strategic, or physical-access results.

## What v0.63 closes

### Exact projection

On the fixed binary fiber, the full-menu RUM set is a capped simplex and
total regularity-violation mass is exactly TV distance to it.

### Exact class moduli

For the complete floor-truncated RUM polygon and every non-RUM completion at
distance at least `gamma`:

```text
Delta=gamma,
Lambda=1+(5/2)gamma.
```

Both moduli have matching primal and dual certificates, explicit ambiguity
at equality, and prospectively verified fresh cells.

### Optimizer support

The multiplicative proof exhausts all feasible violation supports.  It
identifies either singleton with RUM cap `2/5` as the optimizer; supports with
cap sums `3/5` or `4/5` have strictly larger lower bounds.

## Remaining load-bearing sequence

### A. Hidden choice sets and latent selection confounding

Freeze a finite latent consideration-set model distinguishing:

1. recorded menus;
2. bounded but hidden menus;
3. sparse or repeated latent sets;
4. latent states affecting both menu and response; and
5. randomized menu interventions.

The target must be a sharp tier-access corollary or counterexample.  Repeating
a known limited-consideration identification theorem under new names is not
sufficient.

### B. Strategic and dynamic response

Replace a fixed kernel by a responder that observes the query policy or
changes over time.  Freeze whether the responder commits before the
randomized design, whether menu intervention changes the latent target, and
which history is observable to each side.

### C. General modulus computation or complexity

The v0.63 formula exploits a capped-simplex RUM face.  A broader result must
either:

1. give an exact algorithm and dual certificates for a declared family of
   finite RUM polytopes; or
2. prove a sharp complexity barrier for computing the tier moduli.

This is secondary to the hidden-menu target because generic
minimum-distance-to-polytope computation is already classical.

### D. Physical access validation

No theorem shows that a human or model implements the frozen response
channel.  A bridge must prospectively test:

- menu observability;
- conditional stability;
- probability, selection, and recording floors;
- tier separation;
- bounded context degree; and
- downstream decision relevance.

## Claim boundary

The suite does not establish that humans or models possess a coherent latent
value object, that any tier has moral meaning, that the registered interfaces
are physically available, or that the finite rates are practical.  Version
v0.63 closes one exact finite robustness obligation; it does not resolve
ASMP-9.
