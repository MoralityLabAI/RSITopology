# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.54

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.54 closes the declared complete-menu, three-alternative
behavioral-object cell. It separates one Luce ratio scale, a weaker
random-ordering representation, and no representation in the declared strict
random-utility class, with exact certificates and a binary-access obstruction.

The representation mathematics is classical. The resolution gain is that the
ASMP-9 instrument no longer assumes scalar well-posedness in this finite
grammar.

## What v0.54 establishes

For every one of the 1,250 registered kernels, exact rational arithmetic
returns one and only one status:

```text
scalar_luce                           1
random_utility_non_luce             230
no_random_utility_representation  1,019
```

The classical Block-Marschak criterion and an independent exact
ranking-simplex solver agreed on all cells. A uniform scalar kernel and a
no-RUM kernel agree on every binary menu, proving that binary access cannot
decide the endpoint classes.

## What v0.54 does not establish

- A novel stochastic-choice representation theorem.
- A general characterization of which incomplete menu families identify the
  latent-object tier.
- Approximate or finite-sample classification.
- Robustness to ties, weak orders, strategic choice, nonstationarity, or
  context-dependent mixtures.
- That observed behavior carries morally relevant value.
- A calibrated physical preference channel.
- A full ASMP-9 resolution.

## Fixed resolution-directed sequence

1. **Completed through v0.53:** finite decision-relative object; known-channel
   access; finite-sample certification; information/design/modulus
   refinements; deterministic and randomized reporting.
2. **Completed in the declared v0.54 grammar:** finite complete-menu
   scalar/random-utility/no-object classification with exact witnesses.
3. Characterize arbitrary finite incomplete menu systems: when the access
   hypergraph decides the tier, when multiple tiers remain observationally
   compatible, and what additional menu query is necessary.
4. Extend to approximate/finite-sample decisions and to weak, tied,
   context-dependent, or nonstationary demonstrator classes.
5. Extend the access theorem to a compact/continuous experiment class or
   prove a representation-sensitive impossibility.
6. Add correlated/adaptive/strategic misspecification and prove transfer or a
   matching impossibility.
7. Validate or refute a physical preference-access channel prospectively.

## Next load-bearing target

Freeze an incomplete-menu extension problem on finite universes. Given a menu
hypergraph and exact choice laws on its observed menus, classify whether:

```text
all completions occupy one tier;
multiple tiers remain observationally compatible;
or no completion exists in the declared class.
```

The result should characterize access, not merely enumerate another full-menu
grid. It must distinguish:

- identification of Luce weights on connected comparison structures;
- extendability to a random-ordering law;
- and exact separating witnesses when no declared completion exists.

This should again begin with revealed-preference and random-utility prior art,
because partial-domain stochastic rationalizability and random-utility
extension are established research areas.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.54 closes one finite behavioral well-posedness cell using classical
representation theory. It does not close the continuous, incomplete-access,
strategic, physical-channel, or welfare-relevance obligations. ASMP-9 remains
unresolved.
