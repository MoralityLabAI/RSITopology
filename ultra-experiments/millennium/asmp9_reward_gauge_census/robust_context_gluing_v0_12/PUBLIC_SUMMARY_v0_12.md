# Robust context gluing separates repair radius from query conditioning

## Result

Let `G` be the subspace of observations generated by one scalar utility shared
across contexts, and let `L` be the larger subspace generated by a separate
scalar utility inside each context. Because:

```text
G subseteq L,
```

every context-labelled observation `y` has the orthogonal decomposition:

```text
y = P_G y + (P_L-P_G)y + (I-P_L)y.
```

The second term is cross-context non-gluing after the best context-local
scalar fit. The third is within-context non-scalarity. Consequently:

```text
dist(y,G)^2
  = ||(P_L-P_G)y||_2^2 + ||(I-P_L)y||_2^2.
```

This gives an exact Euclidean repair theorem: `dist(y,G)` is the smallest
change that can make the observations compatible with one shared scalar. It
also prevents two distinct failures from being collapsed into one score.

## Query count and robustness are different

Let:

```text
Q = L intersect G^perp
```

be the context-gluing obstruction space and let `q=dim(Q)`. A linear query
design `A:Q -> R^k` identifies the gluing defect only if `k>=q` and `A` has
rank `q`.

Under bounded Euclidean query error, however, the worst reconstruction error
is governed by:

```text
1 / sigma_min(A).
```

Thus the v0.11 count of `q` independent checks remains sharp for
identifiability, but it does not determine robustness. With exactly `q`
row-normalized queries, worst-case amplification is at least one and equals
one exactly for an orthonormal query basis.

## Exact conditioning counterexample

The registered four-item, two-context witness is:

```text
context 0: (0,3), (1,2)
context 1: (0,1), (0,2), (1,3), (2,3).
```

It has six labelled edges and obstruction dimension two. A
minimum-total-support pair of simple mixed cycles uses six edge incidences,
but after equal edge-noise normalization its exact Gram matrix on `Q` is:

```text
diag(2/3, 2/3).
```

Its worst-case amplification is `sqrt(3/2)`. A different pair using eight
edge incidences has Gram matrix `I_2` and amplification one.

Therefore minimum query count and minimum cycle support do not imply robust
optimality, even in the smallest counterexample found by the registered
development census.

## Prior-art and novelty boundary

The projection, Pythagorean, pseudoinverse, and singular-value results are
standard finite-dimensional linear algebra. HodgeRank-style preference
aggregation, minimum cycle bases, and E-optimal experimental design are
established areas. Novelty of those mathematical foundations is not claimed.

The contribution is their specialization to the prospectively registered
context-gluing access grammar, the exact separation between:

- within-context non-scalarity;
- cross-context non-gluing;
- number of checks needed for identifiability; and
- conditioning of the chosen checks;

plus an exact finite counterexample and a fresh registered census.

## ASMP-9 contribution and remaining gap

Version v0.12 improves the robustness obligation within a finite Euclidean
model. It supplies an exact repair radius under a declared norm and proves
that a count-optimal or support-minimal audit need not be noise-robust.

It does **not** resolve ASMP-9. The result assumes:

- a finite common item universe;
- observed context labels;
- a declared Euclidean observation norm;
- linear scalar-difference observations;
- known query maps; and
- deterministic bounded-error geometry rather than a finite-sample
  demonstrator model.

It does not select the norm application-independently, infer latent contexts,
give stochastic coverage, model inconsistent or non-expected-utility
demonstrators, identify reward from sequential behavior, or classify maximal
invariances under a unified observation family.
