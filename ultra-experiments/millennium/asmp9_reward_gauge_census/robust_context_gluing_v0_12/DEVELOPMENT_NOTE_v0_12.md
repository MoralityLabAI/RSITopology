# ASMP-9 robust context-gluing development note v0.12

## Purpose

Version v0.11 established exact existence and exact query count. Version v0.12
asks whether those checks are stable under error.

The development target distinguishes:

- **model validity:** how much observation mass lies outside every
  context-local scalar model;
- **context compatibility:** how much locally scalar mass cannot be generated
  by one shared scalar; and
- **query conditioning:** how strongly a registered mixed-cycle design
  amplifies measurement error.

## Why this is not the proposed generic access lattice

Primary-source review found the generic invariance organization substantially
subsumed by Skalse et al. and classical experiment comparison. The prior-art
gate records that negative scoping result before any v0.12 registration.

## Development program

1. Verify the nested-subspace Pythagorean identity on v0.11 controls.
2. Prove the exact Euclidean repair-radius statement.
3. Prove the `k>=q` access lower bound and the orthonormal-query optimum.
4. Enumerate simple mixed cycles on small context-labelled multigraphs.
5. Compare minimum-total-support and maximum-`sigma_min` bases.
6. Freeze only after identifying whether the cycle-restricted comparison has a
   live positive theorem or a smallest counterexample.
