# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.75 development

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.75 closes the gauge-licensing loop for one declared finite linear
class. The gauge is derived from all policy-occupancy differences, not from the
observation map, and the physical access criterion is then necessary and
sufficient.

## Finite-class closure

finite known policy occupancies,
all cardinal policy margins,

## Claim boundary

This finite-class closure does not validate any premise of the class against
humans, models, or physical environments. It does not settle ASMP-9's broader
five obligations and is not a full resolution.
