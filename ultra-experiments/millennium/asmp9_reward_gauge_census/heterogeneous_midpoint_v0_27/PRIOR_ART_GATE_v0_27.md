5. ASMP-9 v0.26 establishes the full-range offset threshold and the

## What may be claimed

- Cell-specific strictly increasing link shapes do not affect population
  offset-threshold signs when their midpoint is shared and known.
- Arbitrary cell-specific midpoint drift is exactly confounded with utility.
- Context-only midpoint drift reduces to a bipartite incidence model, with one
  gauge per component and cycle rank controlling falsification liveness.
- Laplacian spectral conditioning governs Euclidean quotient stability.

## What may not be claimed

- Rasch connectedness, Hodge residuals, incidence rank, pseudoinverse
  conditioning, or threshold bisection are new.
- Passing a tree design validates the context-only midpoint model.
- Real human or model response links have shared midpoints.
- A practical environment necessarily implements the registered cardinal
  offset.
- ASMP-9 is resolved.
