# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.38

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.38 closes one specific gap in the finite program. It implements
classical deficiency relative to a frozen policy-decision type after a
declared nuisance marginalization and proves an exact query-access threshold
inside one symmetric reward grammar. It therefore separates:

```text
zero-error decision quotient
  < target-relative rule/risk comparison
  < full target-by-nuisance reconstruction.
```

This corrects the mathematical object used for one finite access question. It
does not supply the broad necessary-and-sufficient classification required by
the candidate problem.

## Canonical question

> What preference-query and environment-intervention access is necessary and
> sufficient to identify a reward or value model up to exactly the
> transformations that preserve the declared decision problem?

## What v0.38 establishes

For three reward/decision classes, three policies, symmetric binary target
queries, and a target-independent constant-shift nuisance:

```text
delta_D(one target query, full access)
  = (high-low)/2

delta_D(both target queries, full access)
  = 0

delta_full(both target queries, full access)
  = 1/2.
```

The first equality was prospectively confirmed at `3/14`, `3/8`, and `1/8`.
The gauge observation was target-null under both registered nuisance biases.
All eight gates passed, and an exact replay reproduced the mathematical rows.

This is the first result in the sequence to give a necessary-and-sufficient
access threshold using the decision-relative object that v0.37 identified as
missing.

## What v0.38 does not establish

- A label such as “gauge” does not make an observation statistically
  irrelevant. If representative selection correlates with the target, it can
  leak target information.
- A fixed conditional nuisance law is not robustness to unknown, adaptive, or
  adversarial nuisance.
- The three-policy zero-one regret registry is not a human-value model.
- Symmetric nonadaptive binary queries do not characterize adaptive
  preference/intervention access.
- The Qwen measurement bridge remains separate and unvalidated for this
  object.

## Fixed resolution-directed sequence

1. Extend v0.38 from target-independent to registered target-correlated
   nuisance, and distinguish observational leakage from interventions that
   randomize or set the nuisance.
2. Characterize when target-relative deficiency has a channel/garbling,
   quotient, convex-order, or other scientific representation for a broader
   finite policy-loss type.
3. Derive matching adaptive query and sample-complexity bounds for recovering
   that comparison object rather than the raw reward vector.
4. Add a frozen misspecification neighborhood and prove either robust
   transfer or a matching impossibility.
5. Extend the scalar-versus-relation-versus-no-object trichotomy.
6. Re-enter real-model measurement only through a prospectively registered
   channel that passes availability and calibration gates.

## Claim boundary

Blackwell comparison, Le Cam/Torgersen relative deficiency, and
Goel-DeGroot nuisance marginalization are classical. Version v0.38 contributes
an exact reward-access specialization and threshold. Neither it nor the
accumulated finite suite resolves ASMP-9.
