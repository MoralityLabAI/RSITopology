# ASMP-9 structured-choice-stack prior-art gate v0.61

## Verdict

No component of v0.61 is presented as a new general theory.  The candidate
residual is a versioned composition of classical tools into one conditional
access ledger for the Luce/RUM/non-RUM tier object.

## Bounded contextual choice

Batsell and Polking supply the classical additive expansion of pairwise
log-market-share ratios.  Seshadri, Peysakhovich, and Ugander give a modern
contextual-choice hierarchy with identifiability and sampling results.

- Richard R. Batsell and John C. Polking, “A New Class of Market Share
  Models,” *Marketing Science* 4(3), 1985.
  <https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.4.3.177>
- Arjun Seshadri, Alex Peysakhovich, and Johan Ugander, “Discovering Context
  Effects from Raw Choice Data,” PMLR 97, 2019.
  <https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/seshadri19a.html>

The v0.57 maximum-menu threshold is a direct Boolean-interpolation consequence
inside this classical representation.  A targeted search did not locate the
same fixed-universe Luce-versus-non-RUM sharpness statement, but absence from
that search is not evidence of novelty.

## Testing and concentration

Le Cam’s two-point method, Pinsker’s inequality, Hoeffding bounds, and
multinomial concentration are classical.  The v0.58 rate is a transparent
sufficient ledger, not a minimax constant.

- Bin Yu, “Assouad, Fano, and Le Cam,” 1997.
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-1880-7_29>
- Tsachy Weissman et al., “Inequalities for the L1 Deviation of the Empirical
  Distribution,” HPL-2003-97, 2003.

## Robust contamination

Huber contamination and robust hypothesis testing are classical.  The exact
finite-alphabet overlap formula is elementary.

- Peter J. Huber, “Robust Estimation of a Location Parameter,” 1964.
  <https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177703732>
- Peter J. Huber and Volker Strassen, “Minimax Tests and the Neyman-Pearson
  Lemma for Capacities,” 1973.
  <https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176342363>

## Selection and missingness

Ignorable and nonignorable missingness, choice-based sampling, and endogenous
or unobserved choice sets are established literatures.

- Donald B. Rubin, “Inference and Missing Data,” 1976.
  <https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/63.3.581>
- Charles F. Manski and Steven R. Lerman, “The Estimation of Choice
  Probabilities from Choice Based Samples,” 1977.
  <https://doi.org/10.2307/1914121>
- Joel L. Horowitz, “Modeling the Choice of Choice Set in Discrete-Choice
  Random-Utility Models,” 1991.
  <https://doi.org/10.1068/a231237>

## Frozen claim boundary

Passing v0.61 may support only the conditional finite theorem stack and its
proof handoffs.  It is not a novelty result, an efficient model-selection
algorithm, an empirical preference finding, a welfare theorem, a strategic
or nonstationary result, or an ASMP-9 resolution.
