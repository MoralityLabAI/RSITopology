# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.53

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.53 supplies the exact finite aggregate-randomization LP and proves
that the subset-threshold table sufficient for deterministic Buehler
optimization is insufficient for randomized optimization. This closes the
finite reporting seam opened by v0.51–v0.52.

The remaining work is no longer primarily evidence-order optimization. It is
the canonical value-object problem: continuous and strategic transfer,
physical access validity, behavioral misspecification, and the
scalar-versus-relation-versus-no-object classification.

## What v0.53 establishes

For a full finite probability experiment, aggregate randomized direct
confidence is an exact rational LP. Its basic feasible solutions use at most
`n+k` positive report atoms.

The deterministic subset table is not sufficient for that LP. The registered
family had:

```text
10 experiments
1 common deterministic subset table
1 common deterministic optimum
10 distinct randomized optima
0 primal, dual, table, or support mismatches.
```

## What v0.53 does not establish

- Operational acceptability of randomized safety certificates.
- Continuous or statistic-independent asymptotic transfer.
- Robustness to correlated, adaptive, or strategic misspecification.
- A calibrated physical preference channel.
- Existence of a coherent scalar latent value.
- A complete scalar-versus-relation-versus-no-object classification.
- A full ASMP-9 resolution.

## Fixed resolution-directed sequence

1. **Completed through v0.53:** finite decision-relative object; static and
   adaptive known-channel characterization; finite-sample certification;
   information/design/modulus refinements; deterministic direct completeness;
   randomized minimax regret over valid orders; and aggregate randomized
   coverage over the full finite experiment.
2. Classify finite demonstrator well-posedness: scalar value, preference
   relation/random-utility replacement, or no object in the declared class.
3. Extend the access theorem to a compact/continuous experiment class or prove
   a representation-sensitive impossibility.
4. Add correlated/adaptive/strategic misspecification and prove transfer or a
   matching impossibility.
5. Validate or refute a physical preference-access channel prospectively.

## Next load-bearing target

Freeze a finite stochastic choice grammar with menu-conditioned response laws.
The successor should classify:

```text
scalar expected-utility/softmax rationalizable
relation or random-utility rationalizable but not scalar in the first class
not rationalizable in either declared class.
```

It must begin with revealed-preference, random-utility, and stochastic-choice
prior art. The contribution cannot be “inconsistent choices exist”; it must be
an exact access-sensitive classification or a sharp obstruction.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.53 is a finite specialization of classical randomized confidence
theory. It closes a reporting instrument, not the demonstrator
well-posedness, physical access, or robust identifiability obligations. ASMP-9
remains unresolved.
