# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.64 development

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.64 is an unregistered theorem-development lane.  It narrows the
hidden-consideration obligation to an exact finite separation-cover object,
but it does not add claim-eligible evidence and does not validate any
behavioral or physical premise.

## What v0.64 adds

### Attainable-choice support

For intervention `z` with allowed hidden consideration sets `Gamma_z`, define

```text
O_z(theta) = {max_theta(C): C in Gamma_z}.
```

Under arbitrary preference-dependent compliance, the attainable response laws
form the simplex on `O_z(theta)`.

### Exact finite access criterion

Two deterministic rankings are uniformly separated by `z` iff their
attainable-choice supports are disjoint.  A suite identifies all rankings iff
every ranking pair is separated by some intervention.  Minimum intervention
design is therefore an exact set cover over ranking pairs.

### Endpoint corollaries

- If every singleton remains admissible, all rankings have the same
  attainable-choice support and no preference functional is identified.
- If each intervention forces one exact pair, every pair must be queried in
  the worst-case nonadaptive design, giving the sharp count `C(n,2)`.

The development census checks 872 singleton-collapse rankings, 5,912 complete
pair signatures, 1,098 query graphs, 84 adjacent-swap witnesses, and 7,329,146
ranking-pair/compliance comparisons.

## Registration decision

The [registration decision](hidden_consideration_boundary_v0_64/REGISTRATION_DECISION_v0_64.md)
keeps this result unregistered.  Its components are elementary finite
consolidations beneath mature limited-consideration, latent-choice, comparison
query, and stochastic-choice identification theory.  Re-running known
deterministic cells behind a prospective seal would not close a load-bearing
ASMP-9 uncertainty.

## Remaining load-bearing sequence

### A. Structured hidden consideration

The intermediate class remains open.  A successor must declare restrictions
on `Gamma_z`, dependence between preferences and consideration, repeated or
sparse latent sets, and whether compliance is observed.  It must then compute
or bound the separation-cover object without reverting to unrestricted
singletons or perfect pair forcing.

### B. Strategic and dynamic response

Replace the fixed kernel by a responder that observes the query policy or
changes over time.  Freeze:

1. whether the responder commits before or after the randomized design;
2. which histories are visible to the responder and experimenter;
3. whether the intervention changes the latent target;
4. whether the target is static, drifting, or chosen strategically; and
5. the decision loss under which two latent objects count as equivalent.

### C. General modulus computation or complexity

Extend v0.63 from one capped-simplex fiber to a declared family of finite RUM
polytopes with exact dual certificates, or establish a sharp complexity
barrier for the tier-modulus computation.

### D. Physical access validation

No theorem shows that a human or model implements the frozen response channel.
A prospectively registered bridge must test menu observability, conditional
stability, compliance, selection and recording floors, tier separation,
bounded context degree, and downstream decision relevance.

## Claim boundary

The suite does not establish that humans or models possess a coherent latent
value object, that assigned prompts control actual consideration, that any
finite response tier has moral meaning, or that the declared interventions
are physically realizable.  Version v0.64 is a development-level access
ledger, not a resolution of ASMP-9.
