## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.56 generalizes the v0.55 three-alternative access lattice to every
finite alternative universe with at least three alternatives.  Under
unrestricted positive completion, complete nontrivial-menu access is necessary
and sufficient to identify a singleton Luce/RUM/non-RUM tier.

The limited-domain RUM and arbitrary-menu Luce ingredients are classical.  The
resolution gain is the exact general finite access boundary.

## What v0.56 establishes

For every finite `n>=3`:

```text
proper domain + no RUM completion      -> {N}
proper domain + RUM but no Luce        -> {R,N}
proper domain + Luce                   -> {L,R,N}
complete domain                         -> one observed tier
```

The proof is constructive for non-RUM completions and dimensional for positive
RUM non-Luce completions.

## What remains open

- Which non-circular structural assumptions on unqueried responses permit
  tier certification from a proper domain?
- What are the sharp finite-sample and approximate decision rates?
- How do zeros, ties, incomplete or nontransitive relations change the object?
- What survives endogenous menus, hidden alternatives, context dependence,
  strategic response, and nonstationarity?
- Can the finite theorem be extended to a compact/continuous experiment class,
  or is there a matching representation-sensitive impossibility?
- Which physically realizable access channels measure anything morally or
  decision-relevantly identifiable?

## Next load-bearing target

The unrestricted-completion no-go is now general finite.  The next theorem
must classify assumption families `C` connecting observed and unobserved menu
responses:

> For which non-circular completion classes `C` does a proper observed domain
> identify the Luce/RUM/non-RUM tier of every full kernel in `C`, and what is
> the minimum such domain?

The assumptions cannot simply state that every completion is Luce or RUM.
Candidate classes include bounded-order cross-menu identities, regularity plus
registered higher-order constraints, and known low-dimensional response links.
The result should return both a positive certification condition and a
matching ambiguity witness below it.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.56 closes the arbitrary finite strict-positive unrestricted-
completion tier boundary.  It does not close approximate, continuous,
behavioral, strategic, physical-channel, or welfare-relevance obligations.
ASMP-9 remains unresolved.
