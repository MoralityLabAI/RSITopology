# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.55

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.55 closes the incomplete-menu access lattice for the declared
three-alternative unrestricted-completion grammar. It shows that existential
rationalizability and identification of the full-kernel latent-object tier
are distinct: proper-domain data can refute RUM, but cannot certify the unseen
full kernel as RUM without additional assumptions.

The underlying Luce and ARSP tests are classical. The resolution gain is the
exact access boundary.

## What v0.55 establishes

Across every nonempty menu domain on three alternatives:

```text
proper-domain datasets                     1,125
compatible tiers {L,R,N}                     128
compatible tiers {R,N}                       422
compatible tier  {N}                         575
non-RUM completion failures                    0
positive non-Luce RUM witness failures         0
```

The complete domain recovers the v0.54 singleton classifications.

## What v0.55 does not establish

- General finite-`n` completion ambiguity.
- Approximate or finite-sample tier classification.
- Identification under assumptions connecting unobserved menus to observed
  menus.
- Robustness to endogenous menus, hidden alternatives, ties, strategic
  choice, context dependence, or nonstationarity.
- Moral or welfare relevance.
- A calibrated physical preference channel.
- A full ASMP-9 resolution.

## Fixed resolution-directed sequence

1. **Completed through v0.53:** finite decision-relative objects, known-channel
   access, certification, design, and deterministic/randomized reporting.
2. **Completed in v0.54:** complete three-item
   scalar/random-utility/no-object classification.
3. **Completed in v0.55:** incomplete-menu compatibility and tier
   identification for every nonempty three-item menu domain.
4. Generalize the completion-ambiguity theorem to arbitrary finite universes
   and characterize when structural restrictions on unobserved menus restore
   certification.
5. Add approximate/finite-sample decisions and weak, tied,
   context-dependent, endogenous, or nonstationary demonstrator classes.
6. Extend the access theorem to a compact/continuous experiment class or
   prove a representation-sensitive impossibility.
7. Add correlated/adaptive/strategic misspecification and prove transfer or a
   matching impossibility.
8. Validate or refute a physical preference-access channel prospectively.

## Next load-bearing target

Prove or refute the finite-`n` generalization:

> Under unrestricted positive completion of unqueried menus, every proper menu
> domain that admits a RUM completion also admits a non-RUM completion; and
> every proper-domain Luce-compatible law on `n >= 3` admits a positive
> RUM-but-non-Luce completion.

The first clause should follow constructively from regularity. The second
requires a clean affine-dimension/interiority argument for the random-ordering
polytope and an explicit account of exceptional menu domains. A proof must be
positioned below classical arbitrary-domain ARSP rather than presented as a
new rationalizability theorem.

The useful successor after that is not a larger census. It is a theorem
identifying which additional structural assumptions on unobserved responses
are sufficient to turn existential compatibility into tier certification.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.55 closes one exact finite access lattice. It does not close the
general finite, continuous, approximate, strategic, physical-channel, or
welfare-relevance obligations. ASMP-9 remains unresolved.
