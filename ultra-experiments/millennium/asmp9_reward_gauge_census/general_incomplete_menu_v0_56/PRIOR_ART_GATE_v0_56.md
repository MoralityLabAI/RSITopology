# ASMP-9 general incomplete-menu prior-art gate v0.56

## Verdict

**The rationalizability and arbitrary-menu Luce ingredients are classical.**
The proposed theorem must be presented as an access/identification corollary,
not as a new stochastic-choice representation theorem.

McFadden and Richter characterize random-utility rationalizability for an
arbitrary finite collection of observed choice problems by membership in the
convex hull of deterministic choice types.  Stoye gives a short modern proof.
Falmagne supplies the complete-domain finite random-scale representation.

- Daniel McFadden and Marcel K. Richter, "Stochastic Rationality and Revealed
  Stochastic Preference", in *Preferences, Uncertainty, and Optimality*,
  1990, pp. 161-186.
- Daniel McFadden, "Revealed Stochastic Preference: A Synthesis", 2004,
  <https://eml.berkeley.edu/wp/mcfadden0204/stochastic.pdf>.
- Jorg Stoye, "Revealed Stochastic Preference: A One-Paragraph Proof and
  Generalization", *Economics Letters* 177, 2019, pp. 66-68,
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.econlet.2018.12.023>.
- Jean-Claude Falmagne, "A Representation Theorem for Finite Random Scale
  Systems", *Journal of Mathematical Psychology* 18(1), 1978, pp. 52-72,
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-2496(78)90048-2>.

Alos-Ferrer and Mihm directly characterize Luce choice for an arbitrary
collection of menus and separate falsification, identification, and
out-of-sample prediction.  Their graph-connectivity result is the direct
anchor for the Luce-fiber dimension used here.

- Carlos Alos-Ferrer and Maximilian Mihm, "A Characterization of the Luce
  Choice Rule for an Arbitrary Collection of Menus", forthcoming,
  *Journal of Economic Theory*, accepted version October 2024,
  <https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/226172/1/LogitCharacterizationJET.pdf>.

Kono, Saito, and Sandroni give generalized ARSP conditions when alternatives
inside menus are unobservable.  That is adjacent to, but distinct from, the
present grammar in which entire menus are unqueried and every choice
probability on an observed menu is available.

- Haruki Kono, Kota Saito, and Alec Sandroni, "Random Utility with
  Unobservable Alternatives", *American Economic Review*, forthcoming,
  <https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20240712>.

## Residual statement

The candidate contribution is the following ASMP-9 access distinction:

```text
one Luce or RUM completion exists
```

does not imply:

```text
all unrestricted positive completions belong to that object tier.
```

The proof packages classical full-dimensionality and arbitrary-menu
identification facts into a sharp full-kernel certification threshold.
Novelty is not established; an exact match in the incomplete-choice
literature would subsume the theorem.

## Claim boundary

No novelty is claimed for ARSP, generalized ARSP, Luce cycle conditions,
random-utility regularity, or the geometry of the random-utility polytope.
The candidate theorem concerns full-kernel tier identification under
unrestricted completion.  It does not address samples, approximation,
endogenous menus, hidden alternatives, ties, strategic response, dynamics,
human or model behavior, welfare, or a physical preference-access channel.
