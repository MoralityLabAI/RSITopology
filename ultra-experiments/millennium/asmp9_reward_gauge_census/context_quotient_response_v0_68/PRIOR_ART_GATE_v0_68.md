# ASMP-9 v0.68 prior-art gate

## Verdict

The maximal-invariant and zero-sum contrast results are **classical linear
model facts**. They are not candidate-new mathematics.

The within-groups transformation removes group-specific intercepts in fixed
effects models. Planned contrasts are exactly coefficient vectors that
annihilate the nuisance design. Scheffe's paired-comparison analysis explicitly
handles presentation order and distinguishes subtractive main effects from
order interactions.

Primary anchors:

- Henry Scheffe, "An Analysis of Variance for Paired Comparisons," *JASA* 47
  (1952), 381-400,
  <https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1952.10501179>.
- Yair Mundlak, "On the Pooling of Time Series and Cross Section Data,"
  *Econometrica* 46 (1978), 69-85,
  <https://doi.org/10.2307/1913646>.

The ASMP-9-specific contribution is narrower:

1. identify that v0.67's absolute state sign does not descend through the
   newly declared measurement-nuisance action;
2. state a total endpoint-admission rule using the nuisance design's
   annihilator;
3. keep arm-by-order interactions observable rather than averaging them away;
   and
4. separate a valid context-local response family from the optional hypothesis
   of one global response operator.

This gate must precede any registration. Nothing in v0.68 is presented as a
new fixed-effects, maximal-invariant, paired-comparison, or reward-learning
theorem.
