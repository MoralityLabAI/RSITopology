**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

matrix for one finite shared-channel grammar.  It shows that the independent
generator rectangle materially overstates robust risk, while the exact
integer allocation happens to remain unchanged.  The result does not close
the gap between the confidence-valid upper construction and an intrinsic
minimax lower bound.

## What v0.46 establishes

For each query, the zero-error type and registered likelihood toll induce the
exact shared parameter interval

```text
0 <= p_q <= min(1/2,1-exp(-kappa(n_q))).
```

Blackwell ordering moves the worst experiment to the joint upper corner.
The complete horizon-two four-class policy grammar contains 3,748 exact
degree-two risk polynomials.  Exhausting all 2,080 positive allocations at
total budget 66 gives:

```text
coupled four-class optimum : (28,19,19)
coupled root-group optimum : (64,1,1)
rectangle four-class       : (28,19,19)
rectangle root-group       : (64,1,1).
```

The rectangle exceeds the coupled upper by approximately `0.0930` and
`0.1330` at the respective optima.  A 16-vertex product-family control matches
the rectangle exactly.  Thus shared-parameter coupling, not an implementation
discrepancy, explains the reduced endpoints; integer allocation agreement is a
separate finite finding.

## What v0.46 does not establish

- The method-of-types region is confidence-valid but not shown minimax for the
  final decision deficiency.
- The exact corner theorem relies on iid symmetric binary channels ordered by
  Blackwell garbling.
- The complete horizon-two compiler is exponential rather than scalable.
- Channel misspecification is neither adaptive nor strategic.
- No real behavioral or preference source is present.
- Exact optimizer agreement does not imply that rectangular uncertainty is
  harmless for other budgets, losses, or channel geometries.

## Next load-bearing target

Version v0.47 should separate confidence-set slack from unavoidable
decision-theoretic uncertainty.  Freeze a finite channel family for which the
exact robust upper is computable and require:

1. a declared decision-deficiency modulus over the channel parameter;
2. an explicit least-favourable pair or finite prior producing a lower bound;
3. exact or certified-rational comparison of that lower bound with the best
   coupled upper;
4. a positive fixture where the bounds meet, or a registered constant-factor
   target;
5. a negative control where a convenient two-point construction is known not
   to be sharp;
6. separate reporting of likelihood-region coverage and decision risk; and
7. no claim that a lower bound on parameter radius is automatically a lower
   bound on final deficiency.

The target is a matching finite modulus theorem, not another allocation
census.  If no match is possible in the frozen class, the required outcome is
an exact witness locating the remaining slack.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.46 composes classical method-of-types, Blackwell comparison, finite
minimax, and nonrectangular robust-optimization tools inside one finite ASMP-9
grammar.  It does not resolve general value identifiability, optimal
confidence constants, strategic behavioral misspecification, real preference
access, or ASMP-9.
