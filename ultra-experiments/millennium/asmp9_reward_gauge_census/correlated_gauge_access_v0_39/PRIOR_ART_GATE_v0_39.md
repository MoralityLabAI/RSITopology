# ASMP-9 v0.39 prior-art gate

## Verdict

`classical_specialization_with_new_ASMP9_access_ledger`

No novelty is claimed for experiment comparison, ancillarity, nuisance
marginalization, or stochastic intervention. The candidate contribution is
the exact access distinction inside the frozen reward-gauge grammar:
decision-equivalent reward representatives can have a target-informative
observational assignment law, and the usefulness of that leakage as a query
substitute depends on its target alignment rather than its scalar magnitude.

## Attribution boundary

- Blackwell supplies the comparison/randomization order.
- Le Cam and Torgersen supply deficiencies, including comparison relative to
  a registered decision type.
- Goel–DeGroot supplies the decision-theoretic nuisance-marginalization
  context used in v0.38.
- Pearl supplies the observational-versus-interventional distinction.
- “A statistic is ancillary when its distribution is parameter-independent”
  is textbook statistics, not a contribution here.

The exact half-range computation is a direct finite calculation. The access
alignment table is an ASMP-9-specific specialization, not a claim to a new
general theorem.
