# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.37

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

The exact finite program has established many restricted access, quotient,
stability, design, and impossibility results. Version v0.36 showed that raw
nuisance confusability need not be transitive and therefore need not be a
group orbit. Version v0.37 now shows that even the connected-component
quotient governing zero-error decisions does not determine approximate
target risk. Full expanded target-by-nuisance deficiency supplies a classical
risk-transfer bound, but can be strictly conservative for target-only losses.

These results correct the hierarchy of mathematical objects required by the
problem. They do not complete the broader classification.

The canonical question remains:

> What preference-query and environment-intervention access is necessary and
> sufficient to identify a reward or value model up to exactly the
> transformations that preserve the declared decision problem?

## What v0.37 adds

The confirmation census covered all `4,096` binary experiments in
`{1/4,1/3,2/3,3/4}^6` under one-sample and population-law semantics.

- One sampled transcript induced one component quotient across the entire
  grid, yet exact target-risk profiles varied by as much as `1/4`.
- Population-law access induced five component quotients; one still contained
  a target-risk spread of `1/6`.
- Full expanded-parameter deficiency was `1/2` for a nuisance-only witness
  whose maximum target-only risk gap was `0`.
- All 100 frozen risk-transfer inequalities held exactly.

The consequence is negative but structural: neither a zero-error quotient nor
full nuisance-sensitive deficiency is, by itself, the exact general answer to
the decision-relative access question ASMP-9 asks.

## Physical bridge status

The v0.34.4 Qwen-0.8B capture completed all 1,944 registered receipts and was
exactly repeatable across two cold starts. It rejected its proposed common
prompt-level ruler. Version v0.35 repairs that measurement channel and remains
an execution-gated, prospectively bindable successor; it has not been promoted
into evidence without a valid run.

The physical bridge and the mathematical classification remain separate.
Passing a finite stochastic ledger does not validate the Qwen measurement
interface, and a failed scalar interface does not refute every possible
reward-like internal object.

## Fixed resolution-directed sequence

1. Define a broad but explicit class of target-by-nuisance experiments and
   target-only decision problems.
2. Characterize the exact decision-relative comparison preorder on that
   class, rather than defaulting to either zero-error components or full
   expanded-state deficiency.
3. Determine when that preorder has an equivalent channel/garbling,
   quotient, convex-order, or scientifically meaningful group-action
   representation.
4. Derive matching adaptive query and sample-complexity bounds for recovering
   the resulting object.
5. Extend the classification to a frozen misspecification neighborhood and
   complete the coherent-scalar versus relation-only versus no-object
   trichotomy.
6. Re-enter real-model measurement only through a prospectively registered
   channel that passes its own availability and calibration gates.

## Near-term theorem target

The immediate target is a finite decision-relative deficiency:

```text
Delta_D(A,B)
  = sup over registered target-only decision problems
      [R_A^* - R_B^*]_+.
```

The next protocol should not merely define this operationally. It should seek
an exact primal-dual characterization, prove the reduction to ordinary
deficiency when nuisance is decision-relevant, identify conditions for a
single target-respecting garbling kernel, and exhibit sharp counterexamples
when no such kernel exists.

This is the smallest next step that advances obligation 1 rather than adding
another example to the same finite ledger.

## Claim boundary

The current corpus is a theorem-and-instrument program with exact finite
results, explicit obstructions, and one failed real-model measurement bridge.
Versions v0.36-v0.37 correct the access object in two restricted nuisance
classes. Nothing here identifies a real model's reward, proves general
preference or experiment comparison, authorizes an intervention, or resolves
ASMP-9.
