## What v0.61 closes

### One registered glossary

The stack now shares one explicit convention for:

- positive finite stochastic-choice kernels;
- `BP_r` contextual log-odds degree;
- maximum menuwise `L1` kernel distance;
- total-variation contamination distance;
- closed margin-separated tier fibers;
- recorded pre-response versus outcome-dependent selection;
- probability-coordinate count `Q(n,r)`; and
- non-passing equality states.

### Compactness and attained moduli

The observed probability floor and interpolation norm give a positive
full-kernel coordinate lower bound.  The structured reconstruction image and
its margin-separated tier fibers are compact, so the continuous cross-tier
objectives attain:

```text
Delta = minimum observed-menu TV separation,
Lambda = minimum symmetric coordinate-ratio separation.
```

The threshold theorems are therefore genuine minima on the registered class,
not unqualified infimum statements.

### Prospective independent verification

The source, cells, protocol, and burned inputs were hashed before execution.
The registration was pushed before the fresh matrix was run.  Primary and
independent implementations agreed on the complete canonical fact table.

## Remaining load-bearing sequence

### A. Evaluate a nontrivial class modulus exactly

The stack characterizes robustness through `Delta` and `Lambda` but only
brackets them for the margin-promised bounded-degree class.  The next theorem
should freeze a small nontrivial rational class and produce:

1. a primal cross-tier witness;
2. a dual lower certificate;
3. equality semantics;
4. an exact optimizer or a proved complexity barrier; and
5. sensitivity across `n`, `r`, `a`, and `gamma`.

Point estimates or one-sided numerical searches are insufficient.

### B. Hidden choice sets and latent selection confounding

Freeze a finite latent consideration-set model distinguishing:

1. recorded menus;
2. bounded but hidden menus;
3. sparse or repeated latent sets;
4. latent states affecting both menu and response; and
5. randomized menu interventions.

The target must be a sharp ASMP-9 tier-access corollary or counterexample, not
a renaming of the existing discrete-choice identification literature.

### C. Strategic and dynamic response

Replace a fixed kernel by a responder that observes the query policy or
changes over time.  The quantifier order must state whether the responder
commits before the randomized design and whether the intervention changes the
target value object.

### D. Physical access validation

No theorem yet shows that a human or model implements the frozen channel.  A
bridge must prospectively test menu observability, conditional stability,
probability/selection/recording floors, tier separation, bounded context
degree, and downstream decision relevance.

## Claim boundary

The accumulated suite does not establish that humans or models possess a
coherent latent value object, that any tier has moral meaning, that the
registered access channels are physically available, or that the conditional
rates are practical.  Version v0.61 is a rigorously registered conditional
finite access ledger, not a full ASMP-9 resolution.
