# ASMP-9 v0.64 registration decision

Decision: **do not prospectively register this development lane**.

## Reason

The result is useful as an access ledger, but the outcome is not a
scientifically uncertain claim that merits a fresh-cell registration:

1. unrestricted hidden consideration collapses preference identification by
   the direct singleton construction;
2. arbitrary finite compliance correspondences reduce to intersection of
   finite probability simplexes and a separation cover;
3. exact pair forcing reduces to classical nonadaptive comparison logic; and
4. the surrounding limited-consideration and stochastic-choice
   identification problems already have mature prior art.

Registering another finite census would verify implementation discipline, but
would not materially advance the ASMP-9 resolution obligation.  The eight
tests and import-independent census are therefore retained as development
evidence only.

## Consequence

Version v0.64 may:

- narrow the hidden-consideration obligation;
- define the exact finite separation-cover object;
- provide counterexamples to claims that randomized instructions alone imply
  preference identification; and
- specify what a future compliance instrument must certify.

It may not:

- be described as a prospectively verified result;
- support a novelty claim;
- identify stochastic preference mixtures;
- validate consideration interventions in humans or models; or
- close the hidden-menu obligation.

The next registered result must address a load-bearing uncertainty: strategic
or dynamic response, a nontrivial structured hidden-compliance family, or
physical access validation.
