# ASMP-9 hidden-consideration prior-art gate v0.64

## Verdict

Do not register a generic claim that hidden consideration causes
nonidentification or that instruments can recover consideration.  Both
directions are already active, technically mature literatures.

The admissible residual is a small exact ASMP-9 access ledger that:

- states the unrestricted collapse explicitly;
- separates random assignment from verified intervention compliance;
- reduces arbitrary finite compliance correspondences to an exact
  attainable-support separation cover;
- gives a sharp deterministic pair-forcing endpoint; and
- hands mixture identification back to the existing identified-set theory.

No novelty claim is authorized.

## Primary anchors

### Structured random consideration

Victor H. Aguiar, Maria Jose Boccardi, Nail Kashaev, and Jeongbin Kim,
"Random Utility and Limited Consideration," *Quantitative Economics* 14
(2023), 71-116:

<https://doi.org/10.3982/QE1861>

They provide a unified theoretical and statistical framework for random
consideration with preference heterogeneity, including recovery of preference
and consideration primitives under declared model restrictions.  Version
v0.64 therefore cannot claim that such recovery is new.

### Observational equivalence and attention interventions

Roy Allen, "Exogenous Consideration and Extended Random Utility,"
arXiv:2405.13945:

<https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13945>

Allen proves observational equivalence results for bounded utility shifters,
a discontinuity in consideration-probability identification, and severe
welfare ambiguity for attention interventions.  The v0.64 singleton collapse
is an elementary finite endpoint below this broader analysis.

### Latent choice constraints and instrument separation

Nikhil Agarwal and Paulo Somaini, "Demand Analysis under Latent Choice
Constraints," *Review of Economic Studies* 93 (2026), 2215-2249:

<https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdaf093>

They establish nonparametric identification using distinct instruments for
preferences and choice-set formation and show both are necessary in their
model.  A claim that "two instruments help" would be subsumed.

### Complete identified sets for stochastic choice

Peter Caradonna and Christopher Turansick, "Identification in Stochastic
Choice," arXiv:2602.19950:

<https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19950>

They characterize observational equivalence through Ryser swaps across a
broad family of stochastic-choice models.  Even after full-consideration
forcing, a distribution over rankings is generally an identified set, not a
point.  Version v0.64's positive endpoint is therefore restricted to one
deterministic stable ranking.

## Residual ASMP-9 question

The useful distinction is operational:

```text
randomized prompt or recorded-menu assignment
  !=
verified intervention on the actual considered set.
```

The former leaves the unrestricted singleton construction intact.  The latter
can expose pairwise preference, but only under stable deterministic response
and exact compliance.  This quantifier distinction is the result's intended
ASMP-9 value.

## Claim boundary

The collapse construction, simplex-support criterion, separation-cover
reduction, and complete-pair threshold are elementary.  This lane does not
supersede any cited identification theorem, identify stochastic preference
mixtures, establish welfare semantics, validate a human/model intervention,
or resolve ASMP-9.
