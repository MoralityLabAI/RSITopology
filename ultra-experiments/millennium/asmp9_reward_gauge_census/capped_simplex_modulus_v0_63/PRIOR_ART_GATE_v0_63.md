## Verdict

Treat the v0.63 result as a finite exact specialization and instrument
calibration, not as a new general random-utility or robust-testing theory.

The direct literature already supplies:

- finite random-utility rationalizability as a linear feasibility/polytope
  problem;
- statistical testing through distance to the RUM cone/polytope;
- computational methods for those minimum-distance problems; and
- classical Huber and likelihood-ratio contamination neighborhoods.

The residual content is the closed-form projection and multiplicative-support
formula on the frozen three-alternative fiber.

## Primary anchors

1. Daniel McFadden, "Revealed Stochastic Preference: A Synthesis,"
   *Economic Theory* 26 (2005), 245-264.
   <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00199-004-0495-3>

   McFadden records the McFadden-Richter/Falmagne finite rationalizability
   characterizations and their linear-program formulations.  Version v0.63
   uses only a three-alternative face of this classical geometry.

2. Yuichi Kitamura and Jorg Stoye, "Nonparametric Analysis of Random Utility
   Models," *Econometrica* 86 (2018), 1883-1909.
   <https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA14478>

   Their testing framework treats stochastic rationalizability as membership
   in a finite cone generated by rational choice types.  This directly
   subsumes any suggestion that "distance to a RUM polytope" is itself new.

3. Bart Smeulders, Laurens Cherchye, and Bram De Rock, "Nonparametric
   Analysis of Random Utility Models: Computational Tools for Statistical
   Testing," *Econometrica* 89 (2021), 437-455.
   <https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA17605>

   Their minimum-distance and column-generation setting is the relevant
   computational parent.  The v0.63 capped-simplex formula is a closed-form
   tiny case, not a competing general method.

4. Peter J. Huber, "Robust Estimation of a Location Parameter," *Annals of

   <https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177703732>

distribution of preference shocks through divergence-based robust
optimization:

<https://doi.org/10.1007/s00199-025-01680-2>

That is adjacent robust-RUM work, but it does not make this elementary
fixed-fiber calculation novel.  Conversely, v0.63 does not address their
