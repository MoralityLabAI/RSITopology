# Prior-art and positioning gate

This experiment is not offered as a new identifiability theorem.

- Reward shaping defines an invariance class for sequential decision rewards;
  the canonical reference remains Ng, Harada, and Russell (1999).
- Partial reward identifiability and misspecification under different access
  models are established topics in inverse reinforcement learning and reward
  learning, including the partial-identifiability program of Skalse and
  collaborators.
- Transport across environments is adjacent to domain generalization and
  invariant-prediction work.  V0.82 does not claim those population guarantees.

The residual contribution is narrower: a prospectively frozen, repeatable
finite-registry instrument for testing whether one quotient response
coefficient calibrated on declared families remains compatible with wholly new
families.  The distinction between a finite registry, a sampled population,
and a theorem is maintained throughout.
