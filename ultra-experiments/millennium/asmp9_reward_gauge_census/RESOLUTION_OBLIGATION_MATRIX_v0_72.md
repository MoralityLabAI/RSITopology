# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.72 development

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.72 converts the exact v0.71 path-factorization obstruction into a
known-variance Gaussian lack-of-fit test with a live residual-rank gate and
an exact finite power threshold.

## Status against the five obligations

| Obligation | Strongest evidence after v0.72 | Status | Missing resolution evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Maximal invariance or identifiability object | v0.71 selects Markov reward versus a minimal endpoint/time-respecting history quotient | **Exact for bounded finite path values** | Stochastic-process, continuous, infinite-memory, strategic, and multi-agent objects |
| 2. Necessary and sufficient access | Edge rank identifies rewards; positive residual rank makes Markov factorization falsifiable | **Exact under a fixed complete Gaussian path design** | Minimum path-query designs, adaptive access, target-changing interventions, and physical acquisition |
| 3. Sharp query/sample/intervention bounds | Exact central/noncentral chi-square law; frozen fixture needs exactly 32 equal repeats/path for 80% power at 5% size | **Exact for one known-variance Gaussian effect** | Unknown variance, composite alternatives, context/dependence, adaptive allocation, and general minimax path complexity |
| 4. Robustness to misspecification | Residual projection directly tests failure of the Markov mean class | **One calibrated model-misspecification test** | Missing-not-at-random paths, heavy tails, drift, dependence, contamination, and strategic responses |
| 5. No-go without a coherent latent value object | Rejected Markov factorization hands off to the exact v0.71 history replacement | **Finite path branch with noisy test addressed** | Relation-, stochastic-kernel-, set-, continuous-process-, and multi-agent replacement classes |

## New distinction

One path may be deleted while preserving full edge-reward rank:

```text
reward coordinates remain identifiable;
residual degrees of freedom fall to zero;
Markov factorization becomes unfalsifiable.
```

This prevents a fitted reward vector from being misreported as evidence that
the Markov reward model was capable of failing.

## Next load-bearing steps

1. unknown-variance and non-Gaussian matching tests;
2. optimal path selection balancing parameter rank and residual liveness;
3. stabilization of the history quotient as horizon increases;
4. physical acquisition of frozen path values; and
5. extension to stochastic-kernel and incomplete-relation replacements.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.72 does not validate Gaussian noise, known variances, complete path
access, the semantic or moral meaning of the values, a human/model reward
object, or ASMP-9 resolution.
