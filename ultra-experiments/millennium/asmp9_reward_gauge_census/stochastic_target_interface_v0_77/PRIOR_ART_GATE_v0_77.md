# Prior-art gate for ASMP-9 stochastic target interface v0.77

Status: **classical finite hypothesis testing specialization**.

## Subsumption

No novelty is claimed for:

- identifiability of a finite target from equality classes of probability
  laws;
- Hellinger/Bhattacharyya affinity bounds on likelihood comparisons;
- union bounds over finite hypotheses;
- equal-prior binary Bayes error and total variation; or
- tensorization/coupling bounds for iid total-variation misspecification.

These belong to classical statistical decision and hypothesis-testing theory.

## Residual ASMP-9 role

The deliverable is a target/gauge access ledger:

1. report population target recovery separately from distributional
   representative leakage;
2. require a positive cross-target gap before finite-sample certification;
3. distinguish a sufficient generic bound from exact fixture complexity; and
4. stress the frozen decoder against a declared law-misspecification radius.

## Relation to earlier versions
