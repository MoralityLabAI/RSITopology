## Attribution boundary

- Adaptive experiment selection and its dynamic-programming formulation are
  classical.
- Identifying decision trees and their computational hardness are classical.
- The two-versus-three query witness is elementary.
- The candidate contribution is the exact ASMP-9 risk-generator compiler,
  the static-versus-adaptive access ledger, and its placement under the same
  registered decision-relative containment criterion as v0.40.

Absence of another identical implementation is not asserted as novelty.
