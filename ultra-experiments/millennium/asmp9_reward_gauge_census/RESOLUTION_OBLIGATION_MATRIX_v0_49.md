# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.49

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.49 converts the v0.48 evidence-ordering null into an exact finite
theorem. For any two declared decision risks and one positive reference law,
a common optimal direct-Buehler ordering exists exactly when the intersection
of their tight-predecessor DAGs contains a maximal chain. A labelled
reachable-set cut certifies incompatibility. A smallest two-outcome,
two-parameter statistical experiment proves that no decision-independent
ordering is universally optimal over unrestricted finite risks, while a
zero-curl ordering gauge gives a structural class with identical optimizer
sets.

The exhaustive census covered all `167` Buehler-admissible monotone tables on
four outcomes and all `27,889` ordered pairs with zero theorem mismatches.
The original count gate failed because it registered the full Dedekind count
`168`; an additive verifier-only repair preserves that failure and corrects
only the admissible-universe adjudication.

## What v0.49 establishes

For a subset-bound table `B_d`, positive weights `w`, and Bellman value

```text
V_d(S)
  = min_(x in S) [
      V_d(S \ {x}) + w_x B_d(S)
    ],
```

an ordering is optimal exactly when every prefix edge is tight. Hence two
risks have a common optimum exactly when the intersection of their tight
predecessor DAGs has an empty-to-full path. Path count equals common optimizer
count, and a blocked reachable-set boundary is a finite incompatibility
certificate.

The unrestricted class has no universal decision-independent order: the
smallest witness uses two outcomes and two parameters and has cross-regret
`1/2` in both directions.

If, for some `a>0`,

```text
h(S) = B_2(S) - a B_1(S)
```

induces a zero-curl edge form `w_x h(S)`, the two ordering costs differ by a
positive affine transformation plus a path-independent endpoint constant.
Their complete optimizer sets are then identical.

The v0.48 confirmation has `1,451,520` common paths but is not
ordering-gauge equivalent: its square equations demand 17 distinct positive
scale ratios. Thus the gauge theorem is sufficient rather than necessary.

## What v0.49 does not establish

- A statistic-independent or asymptotic minimax comparison.
- Continuous experiments or unbounded adaptive horizons.
- Robustness to correlated, adaptive, or strategic misspecification.
- A calibrated physical preference channel.
- A complete coherent-scalar versus relation-only versus no-object
  classification.

## Next load-bearing target

For an ordering `pi`, each comparison

```text
C_d(pi;w) <= C_d(sigma;w)
```

is linear in the reference weights `w`. The next theorem should exploit this
rather than search another parameter grid.

Version v0.50 should:

1. define the exact polyhedral region of positive reference laws for which a
   given ordering is jointly optimal for a declared decision family;
2. prove that robustness over a reference-weight polytope is equivalent to
   checking its extreme points;
3. return either a robust common chain with margins or a finite
   weight-and-objective obstruction;
4. recover the v0.48 compatible and incompatible fixtures; and
5. distinguish an empty robust intersection from a small but positive
   robustness margin.

That result would close reference-law sensitivity inside the finite grammar.
It would still not replace the later strategic-misspecification and physical
measurement obligations.

## Claim boundary

Version v0.49 is a finite consolidation of Buehler ordering, Bellman
optimality, bicriterion path intersection, and discrete exact-one-form
mathematics. Its exact census and smallest control sharpen one ASMP-9
subproblem. They do not identify real values, validate human preference
access, prove a general semiparametric or strategic theorem, or resolve
ASMP-9.
