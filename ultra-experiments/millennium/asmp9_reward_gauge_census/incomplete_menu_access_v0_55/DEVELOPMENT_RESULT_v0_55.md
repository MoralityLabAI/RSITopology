## Interpretation

Existential rationalizability and object-tier identification are different
questions.

Partial data can:

- refute every random-utility completion;
- rule out a Luce scale while retaining a RUM completion; or
- retain a Luce completion.

But under the unrestricted completion grammar, any missing menu permits a
regularity-violating completion. Therefore proper-domain data cannot certify
that the unseen full response law is RUM merely because one RUM completion
exists.

## Prior-art boundary

The exact convex-hull test for existence of a RUM completion is classical
McFadden-Richter ARSP. The Luce ratio test and regularity obstruction are also
classical. The development contribution is the ASMP-9 completion-set ledger
and the distinction between existential fit and identification of the
full-kernel value-object tier.
