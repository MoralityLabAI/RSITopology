## What the synthesis adds

observation map cannot license that gauge by itself. Version v0.75 supplies one
noncircular license:

```text
decision target = all registered policy-value differences
licensed additive gauge = annihilator of all occupancy differences.
```

The physical access channel is then tested against that independently derived
object.

## Prior-art boundary before circulation

1. feature-expectation policy values and apprenticeship/IRL formulations;
2. reward transformations preserving policy ordering or optimality;
3. partial reward identifiability under demonstrations and comparisons;
4. linear inverse problems with nuisance quotients; and
5. downstream regret bounds from quotient reward error.
