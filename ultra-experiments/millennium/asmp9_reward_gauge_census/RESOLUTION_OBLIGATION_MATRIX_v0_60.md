# ASMP-9 resolution-obligation matrix after v0.60 development

## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

Version v0.60 closes a finite selection-timing boundary:

```text
recorded pre-response menu selection
  -> supported conditionals are exactly recoverable;

unknown bounded outcome-dependent recording
  -> exact multiplicative cross-tier radius;

unknown unrestricted outcome-dependent recording
  -> all positive clean kernels are confounded.
```

It also matches the `1/pi` finite-sample dependence of a positive menu
selection floor on one cross-tier slice. The result does not identify hidden
choice sets or latent preference-selection confounding.

## What v0.60 adds

### Support, not frequency, under recorded pre-response selection

When menu identity is observed and the conditional response law is stable,
even a target-dependent unknown menu frequency factors out. Its support
selects the observed menu domain; the v0.56 theorem then gives the exact
compatible tier set.

This result is conditional on the factorization. It does not say that a
selection mechanism correlated with latent response state is ignorable.

### Matched positivity exponent

The positive selection floor `pi` multiplies total sample cost by `1/pi`.
The v0.58 pair produces a joint KL divergence `pi D_gamma`, giving a matching
`Omega(1/(pi gamma^2))` lower rate on the same slice.

### Multiplicative recording radius

If unknown response-dependent recording probabilities lie in `[ell,u]`, two
clean laws have the same complete record law iff their coordinatewise
probability ratios fit within `u/ell`. This yields an exact class modulus
`Lambda`. A separation margin gives

```text
Lambda >= (2+gamma)/(2-gamma),
```

while the explicit RUM/non-RUM witness gives

```text
Lambda <= 1/(1-5gamma)
```

on the three-alternative slice.

## Remaining load-bearing sequence

### A. Review and register the conditional finite theorem stack

Versions v0.57-v0.60 are development-only. A claim-eligible stack needs:

1. external subsumption review;
2. one frozen glossary for kernel class, tier fibers, distances, equality
   states, and selection timing;
3. separately registered population and finite-sample claims;
4. import-independent verification from sealed source; and
5. a clear distinction between decidability and efficient implementation.

### B. Compute or certify the class moduli

Both recent exact theorems are expressed through class moduli:

```text
Delta = cross-tier observed TV separation;
Lambda = cross-tier coordinate-ratio separation.
```

The next computational advance should give primal witnesses and dual lower
certificates for these quantities on fixed finite classes. Point estimates
alone are not enough.

### C. Hidden choice sets and latent selection confounding

The next conceptual target is the interface v0.60 explicitly excludes.
Freeze a finite latent consideration-set model and distinguish:

1. recorded menus;
2. bounded but hidden menus;
3. sparse or repeated latent choice sets;
4. latent states affecting both menu and response; and
5. randomized menu interventions.

Because a large identification literature already exists, the target must be
a precise ASMP-9 tier-access corollary or a sharp counterexample, not a renamed
latent-choice-set theorem.

### D. Strategic and dynamic response

After hidden selection, replace a fixed kernel by a responder that observes
the query policy or changes over time. The quantifier order must state whether
the responder commits before the randomized design and whether interventions
alter the target object.

### E. Physical access validation

No current theorem shows that a human or model implements the frozen response
channel. Any bridge must prospectively test:

- menu identity and availability;
- conditional stability;
- probability and recording floors;
- separation from tier boundaries;
- context-degree structure; and
- whether the inferred quotient is decision-relevant.

## Next load-bearing target

The program now has two sensible moves:

1. **rigor move:** externally review and prospectively register the
   v0.57-v0.60 theorem stack; or
2. **scope move:** develop v0.61 for hidden consideration sets with a frozen
   finite latent-selection grammar and a randomized-intervention boundary.

The rigor move should take precedence before another long theorem chain.
Further development is useful only if it attacks a resolution obligation not
already represented by classical theory.

## Claim boundary

The accumulated suite does not establish that humans or models possess a
coherent latent value object, that any recovered tier has moral significance,
or that the registered access channels are physically available. It remains a
collection of exact finite access boundaries, not a full ASMP-9 resolution.
