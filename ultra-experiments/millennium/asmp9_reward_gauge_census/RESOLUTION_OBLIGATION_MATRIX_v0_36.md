## Verdict

**ASMP-9 remains unresolved.**

The exact finite program has established a large collection of access,
quotient, stability, design, and impossibility results. The first registered
real-model bridge then falsified its own scalar measurement channel. Version
0.35 repairs the instrument and prepares a prospectively bound rerun; it does
not convert that bridge into evidence before execution. Version 0.36 proves
that an unknown nuisance can induce a nontransitive confusability graph, so a
group orbit is not always the correct raw access object. Its connected
components still form the quotient governing fixed decision feasibility.
Even together these results do not complete the broader obligations.

> What preference-query and environment-intervention access is necessary and
> sufficient to identify a reward or value model up to exactly the
> transformations that preserve the declared decision problem?

## Physical bridge status

The v0.34.4 Qwen-0.8B capture completed all 1,944 registered receipts and was
exactly repeatable across two cold starts. It nevertheless rejected its common
prompt-level ruler: only 10 of 36 standard curves bracketed, and no mixture
residual was admissible. This is evidence against that measurement interface,
not against the existence of reward-like internal structure.

The v0.35 retrospective theorem replaces violation counts with the exact
distance

```text
d_inf(y,C) = 0.5 * max_{i<j} max(y_j-y_i,0)
```

to the cone of nonincreasing curves. It localizes the remaining defect to
endpoint support and channel bias. The frozen v0.35 protocol therefore adds:

- a complete presentation-order by response-code factorial;
- independent semantic-equality nulls;
- literal code controls;
- probability-order and anchor-dominance controls;
- explicit probability endpoints `p=0` and `p=1`; and
- two process-separated cold-start epochs.

The live runner rejects any manifest that is not both `registered_not_run` and
`execution_authorized=true`. The preparer then binds the actual model, server,
CUDA library, manifest, protocol, runner, analyzer, wrapper, resource caps,
source artifacts, Git commit, and execution environment before any query.

## Nuisance-confusability refinement

Version 0.36 was prospectively registered and pushed before execution. Its
complete finite census found 12 nontransitive confusability relations among 64
minimal one-query channels and none in the registered smaller-cardinality
controls. Across 4,096 query pairs, the graph edge criterion agreed with
direct zero-error decision decoding in all 222,912 registered checks. There
were 24 channels that identified the target only when one nuisance value was
shared across queries; independently resetting that nuisance destroyed the
calibrating information.

Because group-orbit relations are equivalence relations, these nontransitive
cases cannot be represented exactly by any invariance group. The connected
components nevertheless give the minimal forced-equality quotient governing
whether a fixed decision map is uniformly decodable. The corrected obligation
must therefore separate the nuisance-conditioned confusability graph, its
component decision quotient, and any scientifically meaningful gauge action.
This refines a formulation ambiguity in obligation 1; it does not supply the
broad classification the corrected obligation asks for.

## Resolution-directed theorem sequence

The physical bridge is not the whole problem. In parallel, a full resolution
still requires:

1. a declared broad demonstrator/choice-kernel class and its maximal raw
   confusability object, component decision quotient, and necessary and
   sufficient conditions for a scientifically meaningful group action to
   induce that quotient;
2. a necessary-and-sufficient adaptive query/intervention theorem over that
   class;
3. matching joint sample/intervention lower and upper bounds;
4. stability under a frozen, nontrivial misspecification neighborhood; and
5. a two-sided classification separating coherent scalar quotients from cases
   where only a preference relation, choice kernel, or no stable latent object
   exists.

## Claim boundary

The current corpus is a theorem-and-instrument program with exact finite
results, explicit obstructions, and one failed real-model measurement bridge.
The v0.35 protocol is frozen at the scientific level but has not been bound to
an execution environment or run. Version 0.36 corrects one resolution object
in an exact finite channel class. Nothing here identifies a real model's
reward, validates mixture affinity, authorizes an intervention, characterizes
general nuisance channels, or resolves ASMP-9.
