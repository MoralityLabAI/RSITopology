# ASMP-4 randomness-quantifier prior-art boundary v0.8

## Borrowed tools

The proof uses elementary product probability, the fact that a countable union
of null sets is null, exact finite counting, and a polynomial diagonal
fixture.  These measure-theoretic and combinatorial tools are standard.

## Package contribution

The package contribution is the explicit ASMP-4 quantifier audit, the
countable/uncountable boundary theorem, the zero-rate diagonal fixture, and the
two independent finite-grid/limit harnesses.

It does not claim a new general stochastic-game theorem, noisy-channel
capacity result, or normally-hyperbolic invariance theorem.  It also does not
claim that per-disturbance almost-sure safety is the intended canonical reading.

## Attribution boundary

No external theorem beyond the displayed elementary null-union argument is
used.  Every finite count and limit formula needed by the claim is derived in
the theorem and reproduced in code.
