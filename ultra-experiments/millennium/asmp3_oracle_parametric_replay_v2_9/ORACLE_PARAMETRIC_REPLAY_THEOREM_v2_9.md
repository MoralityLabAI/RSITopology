# ASMP-3 oracle-parametric replay theorem v2.9

## Status and scope

```text
result_status = ideal-semantic replay compiler plus transcript-only separation
parent_result = ASMP-3-TRACE-BINDING-EXTRACTOR-v2.8
supporting_results = ASMP-3-ADAPTIVE-TRANSCRIPT-COUPLING-v2.7, ASMP-3-HONEST-SEARCH-BARRIER-v2.1
interface_mode = WV-FIX with a restartable oracle-parametric runtime
changes_parent_problem = false
```

This theorem replaces v2.5's unexplained ideal-simulation premise by a concrete
runtime contract.  It also proves that a recorded noisy transcript alone does
not satisfy that contract: after the first semantic error, the ideal execution
may enter a branch that the recording never visited.

The contract is stronger than protocol admission in the current typed successor
draft.  This release therefore gives a conditional compiler, not an
unqualified characterization of every `WV-FIX` protocol.

## 1. Oracle-parametric runtime contract

Condition on a nonsemantic seed `kappa`.  A protocol runtime is
oracle-parametric when:

1. every participant and verifier transition is total on legal histories;
2. all semantic information enters through an injected provider `O(a)`;
3. the next semantic class is committed before the provider returns its answer;
4. the same runtime is restartable either as callable kernels or as a fresh
   live interaction;
5. `kappa` is generated by an efficient sampler whose distribution is known;
6. the ideal provider `H(a)` is callable at its declared evaluation cost; and
7. kernel execution, messages, trace writes, and any local emulation are
   explicitly charged.

For composition with v2.8, terminal rejection must additionally be
trace-completely bound to a successful canonical `Refute` call.  Trace binding
is not needed merely to replay a decision, but it is needed to extract the
local witness.

A locally emulated adversarial kernel contributes its full execution time.  A
live external strategy may instead be treated as an online interaction, but
its message and orchestration costs remain charged.  A one-shot stateful
opponent that cannot be restarted is outside this theorem.

## 2. Ideal replay compiler

Let `Run(kappa,O)` be a total oracle-parametric runtime and let `mu` be its
nonsemantic seed law.  Define

```text
Replay_H:
  sample kappa' from mu;
  execute Run(kappa',H);
  emit the canonical execution trace.
```

### Theorem 1 (exact ideal law)

`Replay_H` has exactly the ideal protocol's execution law:

```text
Law(Replay_H) = Law(Run(kappa,H)),  kappa sampled from mu.       (1)
```

Its charged time is

```text
SeedSample + KernelRun + V + q Eval_H
  + TraceWrite + TraceScan + Canonicalize.                       (2)
```

### Proof

For every fixed seed, dependency injection replaces only the semantic provider;
the total transition machine then generates the unique ideal history
`Run(kappa,H)`.  Sampling `kappa'` from the same law `mu` and averaging these
point masses gives (1).  Executing the sampler, kernels, verifier, at most `q`
ideal-provider calls, and the trace operations gives (2).  QED.

The shared seed used in v2.7's pathwise coupling is a proof device.  A finder
does **not** need to recover the seed realized in a previous noisy execution.
An independent seed with the same law has the correct ideal marginal, which is
all the v2.5 rejection-probability argument uses.

## 3. Finder composition

Assume trace-complete binding from v2.8 and adaptive fresh post-history blocks
from v2.7.  If noisy false acceptance is at most `s`, then the compiler samples
an ideal execution, and the trace extractor succeeds with

```text
alpha >= 1-s-delta_q,
delta_q <= 1-(1-e_d)^q.                                        (3)
```

The witness uses at most `q` replication-quotiented semantic classes.  The 12
registered parent rows retain their exact positive success margins.  Their new
resource ledgers add seed sampling, kernel dispatch, and charged local strategy
execution to every v2.8 extractor cost.

Thus ideal-semantic replay is no longer an independent premise for the declared
oracle-parametric subclass.  The remaining premises are the operational
runtime contract itself, trace-complete binding, and the registered noise law.

## 4. Exhaustive adaptive compiler harness

For two and three semantic classes, query depths one through three, the
producer enumerates every complete binary adaptive query tree, every semantic
world, and every vector claim.  This covers:

```text
2,355 complete query policies,
144,096 ideal executions,
429,984 semantic-provider calls,

```

Dependency-injected replay agrees event-for-event with a separately written
direct tree semantics in all 144,096 executions.  All 107,728 rejecting traces
contain a valid successful call and extract a singleton witness; all 36,368
accepting traces remain unbound by a successful refutation.  There are zero
compiler mismatches and zero invalid traces.

A second exact audit uses three seed kernels and every rational three-point
seed law with denominator one through twelve.  Across 29,056 world, claim, and
seed-law cases, independently sampled replay and shared-seed coupling have the
same ideal rejection marginal, with zero mismatches.

Eight single-fault runtime descriptors exercise partial transitions, hidden
semantic side channels, missing ideal access, transcript-only input, opaque
seeds, post-answer query selection, uncharged emulation, and decision-only
rejection.  Both the producer and clean-room checker reject all eight and
accept the callable-kernel and live-interaction positive controls.

## 5. Transcript-only firewall

For each `N`, take a public gate class and `N` marker classes.  World `w_m` has
ideal gate answer one and a unique positive marker `m`.  The candidate strategy
kernel behaves as follows:

- after recorded noisy gate answer zero, emit the same accepting continuation
  for every `m`;
- after ideal gate answer one, emit `m`, causing the verifier to query and
  refute at marker `m`.

All `N` noisy recordings are therefore the same, while the unvisited ideal
branches expose `N` distinct witnesses.  An estimator given only the recording
has minimax marker-identification success `1/N`.  A probe-confirmed extractor
restricted to output one of `k` ideal-probed markers has success `k/N`.  A
restartable oracle-parametric interaction follows the ideal branch and confirms
the marker in two semantic queries, succeeding with probability one.

Rows `N=2,...,20` certify these exact rates.  This is an information boundary,
not a claim that every nonrestartable protocol is hard: it proves only that
restartability cannot be inferred from the extensional recorded transcript.

## 6. Relation to the typed successor

The typed successor declares a protocol tuple of algorithms, but it does not
state that an honest finder may restart every strategic kernel under an injected
ideal provider.  It also does not specify whether adversarial private state is
rewindable, whether a second ideal-mode interaction is legal, or who pays for
local emulation.  Consequently Theorem 1 cannot silently be applied to every
protocol admitted by that draft.

A normative successor can make the compiler automatic by requiring:

- total dependency-injected semantic access;
- a restartable kernel or live ideal-mode interaction interface;
- an efficient nonsemantic seed sampler;
- callable charged ideal evaluation; and
- trace-complete operational `Refute` binding when witness extraction is used.

## 7. Completion boundary

For restartable oracle-parametric, trace-bound protocols under v2.7 fresh
blocks, the v2.3-v2.9 chain now derives the ideal replay, rejection witness,
randomized finder, adaptive coupling, amplification, message, query, and
explicit replay resources.

Still open are one-shot or stateful strategic interactions, hidden semantic
side channels, roles without ideal-provider access, persistent/adversarially
correlated noise, universal interactive resource lower bounds, the normative
`FIX`/`ADM` choice, and attributable external review.

## 8. Novelty boundary

Oracle substitution and equality of sampler marginals are elementary.  The
contribution is their precise ASMP-3 normalization role: the first operational
compiler for v2.5's ideal-simulation premise, explicit strategy-runtime costs,
fresh-seed sufficiency, an adaptive exhaustive implementation audit, and a
finite unvisited-branch separation showing why transcript replay is weaker.
