# Structured observation-kernel motif v0.1

## Deflated statement

Several ASMP instruments share the same elementary linear-algebra skeleton.
For a structured object space `Theta` and a nested observation family `O_r`,
indistinguishable alternatives are differences in `ker(O_r)`. Identifiability
holds when the relevant quotient of that kernel vanishes; sharpness is shown by
an explicit witness in the first surviving graded piece.

This is not a deep conservation law. The abstract statement follows from
rank-nullity. The mathematical content of each instance is the structured rank
calculation, the quotient or gauge, the observation cost, and a witness proving
the threshold sharp.

## What would make a family statement nontrivial

A general rank-nullity slogan adds little. A stronger program would have to
explain or predict which structured filtrations arise from real oversight and
mechanistic-interpretability access, including:

1. why a physical or institutional interface exposes one filtration rather
   than another;
2. how noise, adaptive querying, and gauge ambiguity change its effective
   rank;
3. whether the first invisible graded piece predicts an actual failure mode;
4. how observation cost scales before the kernel becomes operationally small;
   and
5. whether richer observables close the kernel out of sample rather than only
   on planted fixtures.

Those are empirical and complexity-theoretic questions. The current pilots
probe them one instrument at a time; the motif does not answer them.

## External-description rule

Describe the family as “structured rank computations with sharpness
certificates across several safety-relevant observation models,” not as a
unified new theorem. Attribute the classical rank result in every instance and
reserve novelty claims, if any, for the observation model, quotient, cost
criterion, or empirically validated failure prediction.
