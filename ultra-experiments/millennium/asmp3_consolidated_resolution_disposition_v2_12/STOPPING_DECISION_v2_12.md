# ASMP-3 consolidated stopping decision v2.12

Stop extending the existing parity, marker, coupling, trace, replay, and online
observation grids.

This is now justified by two levels of exact evidence:

- v2.6 proved that the original parity/coupling grids could not settle normative
  definition, universal lower bounds, or external review;
- v2.11 proved that the expanded online-extractor architecture itself is
  black-box minimal and supplied exact failure families for every removed
  premise.

The previously legitimate broader-theorem trigger has been used: v2.7-v2.10
removed decision-coupling, witness-transparency, and restartability premises.
V2.11 then closed the resulting online-contract audit.  Another row in those
families no longer increases the quantified scope.

Resume only for one of the five clearing events in the machine artifact:

1. authoritative normative interface adoption;
2. a concrete non-black-box family or universal structure theorem;
3. interface-uniform interactive resource lower bounds;
4. a broader correlated-noise hypothesis with a path theorem or impossibility;
5. attributable external reproduction.

This stopping decision does not retract the exact literal refutation or the
repaired subclass theorem.  It prevents those internal results from being
inflated into an unrestricted or community-accepted claim.
