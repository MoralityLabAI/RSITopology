# ASMP-5A finite tiling prior-art boundary v0.1

The mathematical ingredients of this seed are classical.

- Full binary trees with `k` leaves have `2k-1` total nodes, and minimum depth
  `ceil(log2(k))` is the elementary capacity bound for binary trees.
- The Horton-Strahler number is the register function of a binary expression
  tree. It measures the minimum registers needed under the classical tree
  evaluation model; this is not a new certificate-complexity invariant.
- Brent's parallel evaluation work supplies the broader work-depth framing.
- Tiling Agents and parametric bounded Lob address substantially richer
  reflective systems than this explicit-conjunction grammar.

The bounded contribution is an exact, preregisterable instrument that keeps
work, depth, memory, and semantic coverage separate. It can falsify an
underspecified scalar-degradation story but cannot claim new tree theory.
