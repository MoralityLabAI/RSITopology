# ASMP-10 bounded continuation claim boundary v0.2

## Task result supported by a passing run

A passing report supports the exact formula

`Delta* = 2B |phi(q)| / ||q||_(1,mono)`

for the global pairwise future-score envelope in the frozen first Hermite
kernel. It also supports zero nullity at `D=J-1`, one-dimensional nullity at
`D=J`, exact budget homogeneity, and budget monotonicity for the registered
rational census.

The result is a direct finite-dimensional consequence of classical Hermite
interpolation and L1/L-infinity duality. Novelty is not claimed for either
ingredient.

## Reliability evidence

Reliability comes from exact rational arithmetic, explicit feasible witnesses,
five score-pseudometric probes, and agreement with a structurally separate
closed-form/determinant/dual verifier. These facts support the computation in
the frozen class. They are not empirical evidence about learned systems.

## Claims not supported

This phase does not establish:

- a conditional envelope for any particular nonzero observed prefix;
- a result for noisy jets (`delta>0`);
- an optimum when `D>J` and the invisible kernel is multidimensional;
- invariance under a change of polynomial basis or coefficient norm;
- a theorem for an infinite series or a generic analytic-function space;
- a neural-network loss-curve, grokking, SLT, representation, or capability
  transition theorem; or
- resolution of ASMP-10.

The word *analytic* refers only to the bounded polynomial continuation slice
selected from the successor scope. The frozen coefficient L1 norm is
coordinate dependent, and the basis-materiality control is designed to make
that limitation visible rather than erase it.

## Operational statement

The report builder performs deterministic CPU-exact computation and writes
only to standard output. It does not authorize or launch the GPU training-model
branch, use the network, alter existing v0.1 artifacts, or update shared ASMP
status documents.
