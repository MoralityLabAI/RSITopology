# ASMP-4 stopping red-team prior-art boundary v0.10

## Used material

The logical step is the elementary model-theoretic observation that a text
admitting two completions with different target values does not determine one
value.  The NHIM, local-control, and exact-region facts are inherited from the
sealed v0.6 package and independently replayed here at the fixture level.

The bounded external audit in `EXTERNAL_PRIOR_ART_SCOPE_v0_10.md` reads the
definition pages of all four control-under-information-constraints works cited
by ASMP-4.  Each fixes one charged information resource: a single digital
sensor-to-controller alphabet or a single spanning-set cardinality.  None
defines a separate charged controller-to-actuator transcript or selects the
raw/computed sensor registry used by the canonical two-port target.  Tatikonda
and Mitter additionally show that changing an encoder information pattern can
change the required rate.

The separate targeted audit in `TARGETED_LITERATURE_NEAR_MISSES_v0_10.md`
checks three strong uncited neighbors: a genuine multidimensional network rate
region, payload-versus-timing rates on one event-triggered channel, and a
network IFE composition.  Their coordinates belong to plant subsystems or to
different accounting views of one channel.  None selects ASMP-4's sequential
read/write registry, and each would require an added architecture mapping.

## Nonclaims

This package does not claim a new invariance-entropy theorem, stochastic-game
theorem, or normally-hyperbolic classification of the v0.8 diagonal.  It does
not claim that either sensor completion is the intended ASMP-4 registry, and it
does not claim a full canonical resolution.

The external audit is not an exhaustive literature search and is not external
expert review.  It establishes only that the four works cited by ASMP-4 do not
supply the missing two-port registry selector; an attributable later theorem
could reopen that conclusion.

The targeted search is likewise non-exhaustive.  It rejects only the
applicability of the three inspected near-misses and cannot exclude a different
attributable theorem with the canonical two-port architecture.

Its contribution is an adversarial dependency audit: it removes a scope-
contestable auxiliary witness from the minimal stopping proof and verifies that
the conclusion remains forced by the primary sensor fork.  The thin invariant
set is used only for that exact semantic diagnosis; no perturbation-stable or
positive-volume resolution is claimed.
