# ASMP-4 adversarial stopping argument v0.10

## Why stop survives the scope challenge

The strongest immediate objection to v0.9 is correct but nonfatal: the v0.8
diagonal does not belong to the registered NHIM positive subclass.  V0.10
therefore removes it from the minimal proof.

The remaining certificate is enough.  A rational same-plant NHIM/local-control
fixture has two exact capacity regions depending only on whether the normative
sensor/computation registry permits the action-fiber computation or forces raw
transduction.  Both model builders satisfy all 13 explicit architecture
obligations.  Neither the Markdown nor the non-normative index chooses a
registry.

No larger local census can infer a missing normative predicate.  It can only
classify another declared completion.

Explicit mutation confirms the point: computed-only, relational-only,
raw-only, and union selectors all make the target determinate, but none is in
the source.  The union's computed rectangle is therefore a coherent answer to
a newly selected problem, not an implicit repair of this one.

The compact zero-dimensional `K_0` is permitted by the literal setting; the
positive-volume collar is a liveness example.  Robustness remains an unsatisfied
graduation obligation, so this certificate is a reason to stop and repair the
definition rather than a claim of robust resolution.

## Four reopening conditions

Resume the stopped lane when at least one of the four reopening conditions is
met:

1. the canonical source adds a normative sensor/computation registry;
2. an attributable audit finds an error in the primary sensor fork;
3. a new canonical clause selects one of the existing completions; or
4. a new registered class invalidates the finite completion analysis.

The probability/disturbance-order question remains a separate productive
specification task.  It is not needed to justify this stop.
