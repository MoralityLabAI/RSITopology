## Decision

Stop enlarging the current finite prefix-relay census after v0.5. The
registered v0.4/v0.5 lane is resolved: its complete deadline action universe,

## What the harness has closed

The conclusion no longer rests on a pattern in a small grid.

1. The one-block search is exhaustive over all binary four-word prefix codes
   meeting the three-tick deadline: 207 unlabelled codebooks, 4,968 labelled
   codebooks, 250 causal read/write actions, and 13 coordinatewise minima.
2. The Bellman recurrence is a proof for every finite horizon. Exact central
   values through 12 blocks and an independent implementation through eight
   blocks test the implementation; they are not being used as an induction
   substitute.
3. The finite optimizer has a closed stopping-time formula. The two-block
   adaptive improvement is also exhaustively checked over all 371,293 minimal
   action trees.
4. The asymptotic corner follows analytically from a pathwise write bound and
   a nonnegative exponential supermartingale. It is not extrapolated from the
   finite tables.
5. The same construction is proved for every positive finite i.i.d. plan
   alphabet. A uniform conditional-MGF theorem gives a correlated-source
   extension, while the vanishing-maximum theorem covers bounded negative-drift
   processes even when no uniform one-step multiplier exists. Time-varying and
   observable stationary-ergodic sources separate those hypotheses. The finite
   probability grids exercise these proofs but do not define their scope. A
   general theorem now classifies every stationary ergodic finite full-support
   source with a common sufficient predictor; a finite positional mean-payoff
   theorem handles state-dependent support, including an exact fixture where
   expected- and worst-optimal code policies are distinct.
6. A separately implemented verifier reconstructs the codebooks, actions,
   Bellman values, strategy census, finite-alphabet profiles, and correlated
   Markov fixture without importing the central harness. All earlier ASMP-4
   verifiers and the frozen v0.1 receipt replay remain green.

Therefore another horizon, probability denominator, or four-state Markov grid
would add regression coverage but would not change the registered theorem.

## Why this is the correct stopping boundary

The canonical ASMP-4 problem ranges over registered nonlinear controlled
processes with disturbances, evaluator-transversal quotients, memory, delay,
control authority, and worst-case transcript rates. The v0.5 harness is a
finite prefix identity-relay boundary model with an expected-read successor
cost. No amount of enumerating more instances inside that model can establish
a variational theorem for every canonical plant class, nor can it decide the
normative question of which architecture registrations should be admitted.

Progress beyond v0.5 therefore requires a new mathematical input, not a larger
version of the same census: an external proof audit of the v0.2/v0.3 canonical
reduction, a genuinely new registered architecture with a separating
counterexample, or a theorem for a broader control/source class. The prize-style
requirement of reproduction by independent expert teams also cannot be
satisfied by more local runs of one repository.

`CANONICAL_SCOPE_AUDIT_v0_5.md` performs the available internal quantifier
audit. It finds the bilateral proof sound under its written computation-
mobility premises and isolates the remaining interpretation questions that a
local finite harness cannot adjudicate.

## Conditions that would reopen this harness lane

Reopen rather than stop if any of the following occurs:

- an independently implemented checker disagrees with a registered count or
  Bellman value;
- a counterexample violates the truncation, causal-action reduction, guarded
  optimizer, conditional-MGF, or vanishing-maximum proof under its written
  assumptions;
- external review identifies an uncharged timing, shared-state, or schedule
  channel in the construction;
- the task registers a materially different source, port cost, alphabet,
  finite-slack convention, or controller architecture; or
- the canonical v0.1 quantifiers are sharpened enough to make a new reduction
  or counterexample obligation precise.

Absent one of those triggers, the evidence-backed position is:

- **resolved inside the v0.4/v0.5 registered fixture:** exact finite adaptive
  frontier and asymptotic rectangle;
- **candidate/partial at canonical ASMP-4 scope (historical v0.5 label):** the
  v0.2/v0.3 reduction and replacement characterization needed mathematical and
  scope review; v0.6 now makes the missing computation-closure registration
  explicit; and
- **not justified:** claiming that further finite prefix-code enumeration
  would resolve the remaining nonlinear-control problem.
