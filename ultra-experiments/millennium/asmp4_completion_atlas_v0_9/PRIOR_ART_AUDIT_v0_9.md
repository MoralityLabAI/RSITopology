# ASMP-4 completion-atlas prior-art boundary v0.9

## Logical tool

The semantic observation used here is elementary: if two models satisfy the
same explicit axioms but assign different values to a target, those axioms do
not determine the target.  Model-theoretic underdetermination is not claimed as
a new idea.

## Package contribution

The contribution is the sealed ASMP-4 application: direct requirement
extraction, seven-claim evidence mapping, two independent model-completion
axes, mutation sensitivity, cryptographic integrity, and an executable
reopening contract.

This package does not claim a new general theorem in control theory,
information theory, probability, or model theory.  It does not claim that any
one source completion is the author's intended problem, and it does not claim
external peer acceptance.
