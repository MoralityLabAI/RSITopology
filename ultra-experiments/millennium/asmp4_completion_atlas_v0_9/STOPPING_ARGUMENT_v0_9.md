# ASMP-4 consolidated stopping argument v0.9

## Decision

Stop local enumeration and request normative registration.  The current
source does not determine a unique code domain or randomized safety predicate,
and exact same-plant models prove that both omissions change the target.

## Why the harness is sufficient for this decision

The stop is not inferred from elapsed effort.  Eight sealed resources,
seven exact predecessor claims, five canonical completion requirements,
three sensor targets, two stochastic outcomes, 112 predecessor tests, and two
independent atlas implementations all agree on the same boundary.

The central atlas also mutates each missing selector.  A computed/raw sensor
clause selects different rate regions; a support/per-path stochastic clause
selects different feasibility.  The base source selects neither.

## What remains mathematical

Once a normative completion is supplied, substantial theorems are already
available: diagonal normal-form regions, heterogeneous and adaptive prefix
frontiers, finite graph entropy games, nonunique-action tradeoffs,
support-kernel derandomization, and countable/uncountable stochastic
boundaries.  A newly declared class outside those reductions would be a valid
next mathematical target.

## What no harness can supply

A harness cannot decide what “registered causal code” was meant to quantify
over or whether random safety means per-path, uniform, or support zero error.
Those are definition choices.  Treating one as implicit would violate the
source's own rule that domains, randomness, adversaries, and quantifier order
be explicit.

## Reopening contract

Resume only for a normative sensor registry, normative stochastic order, new
unreduced registered class, or attributable external proof error.  Merely
increasing horizon, state count, alphabet size, or Monte Carlo budget does not
meet this contract.
