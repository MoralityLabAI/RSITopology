# Prior-art boundary v0.29

Feedback and invariance entropies as operational data-rate quantities are
classical. Nair, Evans, Mareels, and Moran,
[Topological Feedback Entropy and Nonlinear Stabilization](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2004.834105),
connect topological feedback entropy to communication-limited stabilization.
Tomar, Rungger, and Zamani,
[Invariance Feedback Entropy of Uncertain Control Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.05242),
develop a history-dependent data-rate notion and an uncertain-system data-rate
theorem. No novelty is claimed for feedback entropy, invariance entropy,
prefix-tree successor products, causal tree maps, or elementary fiber-counting
inequalities.

The v0.3 repository theorem already registers the exact worst-path branching
metric `B_T`, separates it from terminal language cardinality and sequential
prefix-free cost, and warns that a generic deterministic causal map need not
obey naive pointwise data processing. V0.28 then proves a terminal-language
factor theorem using full-word fibers.

The v0.29 contribution is the missing ASMP-4 factor law for `B_T`:

1. local successor fibers are multiplied along causal port paths;
2. that product gives a finite-horizon and asymptotic two-port correction;
3. a repeated injective-terminal disclosure block proves that terminal fibers
   can be zero-entropy while local branch-fiber entropy is positive;
4. a clone family and sparse/burst schedules establish sharpness and the
   subexponential/limsup boundary; and
5. central exhaustive and import-independent morphism censuses test the exact
   quantifiers and reject eight weakened definitions.
