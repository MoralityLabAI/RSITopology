## Negative-resolution checklist

The v0.1 general protocol permits “an undecidability result for the frozen
uniform decision family” as a resolution. The prize protocol further requires
that the associated family—not merely a narrower subclass—be undecidable.

V2.20 establishes:

- a total computable reduction from `NONHALT`;
- strict fixed message, order, stopping, and query interfaces;
- efficient honest work and `polylog(T)` verifier resources;
- nonzero fully specified noise;
- local atoms with a constructive full-answer firewall;
- exact positive and negative per-depth values; and
- an independent reconstruction of all finite mechanics.

It does not establish that `WV-FIX-UCOMP` is the unique “associated” family
intended by v0.1. This missing premise cannot be supplied by another finite
fixture. It requires an authoritative representation freeze or a reduction
showing that every intended parent instance is captured by the registered
encoding.
