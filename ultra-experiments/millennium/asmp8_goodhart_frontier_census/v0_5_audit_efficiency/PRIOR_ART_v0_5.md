# ASMP-8 v0.5 prior-art boundary

- Maurer, A. and Pontil, M. (2009), *Empirical Bernstein Bounds and Sample
  Variance Penalization*, COLT 2009,
  <https://www.learningtheory.org/colt2009/papers/012.pdf>. Theorem 4 supplies
  the registered variance-adaptive upper bound.
- Serfling, R. J. (1974), *Probability Inequalities for the Sum in Sampling
  without Replacement*, Annals of Statistics 2(1),
  <https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176342611>. This is the classical
  finite-population concentration anchor.
- The movement-ordered bound is an elementary robust partial sum: observed
  terms are exact and each unseen term is assigned its support-function worst
  case. No novelty is claimed for this construction.

The experiment-specific contribution is the matched first-passage comparison
under one Goodhart registry and one prospectively sealed receipt chain.
