# Prior-art boundary for the ASMP-2 source-fiber theorem

This packet does not claim novelty for its classical components.

## Local semiparametric factorization

Van der Vaart's
[On Differentiable Functionals](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176347976)
established the Hilbert-space differentiability/information framework.
Trabs'
[Information Bounds for Inverse Problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.6610)
uses generalized score operators and adjoints for regular indirect models.
Pérez-Izquierdo's April 2026
[Semiparametric Fisher Information in Models Parametrized by a Normed Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00655)
states explicitly that positive semiparametric information is equivalent to
the functional gradient lying in the range of the adjoint score operator,
extending the Hilbert result to normed spaces.

Accordingly, ASMP-2's local factorization clause is treated here as prior art,
not as the new result.

## Statistical experiments and minimax moduli

Blackwell's
[Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032)
characterizes experiment informativeness through decision risks. The
source-fiber value is a safety-decision-specific specialization, not a new
general comparison-of-experiments theorem.

Donoho and Liu's
[Geometrizing Rates of Convergence II](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176348114)
relates minimax functional-estimation rates to Hellinger moduli. The present
infinite-sample lower bound is the zero-distance endpoint: the hard worlds
have identical source experiments but different safe-action sets.

## Smooth continuation

Le Gruyer's
[Minimal Lipschitz Extensions to Differentiable Functions Defined on a Hilbert Space](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-009-0027-1)
gives sharp `C^{1,1}` extension machinery for fields of affine jets. The bump
construction here is elementary and does not replace that theory. Its role is
to show that qualitative smoothness or a finite curvature ceiling without a
positive safety margin cannot make finitely many source environments globally
determining.

## Candidate contribution

The candidate-new part is the exact assembly of:

- globally safe-and-useful action sets on source observational fibers;
- the fiber maximin value as the certification boundary;
- a smooth QMD, positive-Fisher, individually feasible opposite-action witness;
  and
- the density/finite-active-design obstruction tied directly to ASMP-2's
  resolution quantifiers.

External expert review is required before treating that assembly as novel or
as a full negative resolution.
