# ASMP-6 v0.1 prior-art addendum

The frozen prior-art boundary is retained. For circulation, the primary
anchors are:

- Christian Cachin, *An Information-Theoretic Model for Steganography*
  (Information Hiding 1998; Information and Computation 2004). Cachin's
  hypothesis-testing convention uses `D(P_cover || P_stego)`, which is why the
  protocol registers the cover-first chi-squared support rule separately from
  the reverse direction.
- Gustavus Simmons, *The Prisoners' Problem and the Subliminal Channel*
  (CRYPTO 1983 proceedings, published 1984), for the covert-coordination
  setting.
- Classical constrained arbitrarily varying channel work for active channel
  state selection and state budgets.
- Steganographic and low-probability-of-detection square-root-law work for the
  asymptotic regime expressly outside this census.

The exact four-symbol frontier is a specialization and instrument test. No
novelty is claimed for finite hypothesis testing, deterministic channel
perturbation, chi-squared domination of KL, or classical covert capacity.
