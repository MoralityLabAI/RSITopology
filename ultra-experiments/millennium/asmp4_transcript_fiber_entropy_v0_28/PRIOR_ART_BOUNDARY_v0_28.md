# Prior-art boundary v0.28

Entropy under factor maps and the entropy carried inside factor fibers are
classical subjects. Ledrappier and Walters,
[A Relativised Variational Principle for Continuous Transformations](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-16.3.568),
develop the relative-entropy framework for topological factors and build on
Bowen's factor/fiber entropy inequality. No novelty is claimed for relative
topological entropy, factor entropy, finite-to-one factor ideas, or the
cardinality inequality `|domain| <= max_fiber * |image|`.

The earlier repository packages also delimit the claim. V0.3 already proves
bilateral relay normal forms for shared transcript-tree costs when the
transformed trees are isomorphic. V0.20 proves that raw-symbol cloning shifts a
forced-raw language rate by `log2 m` while leaving the control-relevant support
unchanged. V0.26 transfers additive path costs through exact public-history
bisimulation.

The v0.28 contribution is the synthesis specific to ASMP-4:

1. separate read/write language costs are placed on transferred safe strategy
   trees rather than on a single autonomous dynamical system;
2. port-compatible causal factor maps and directional maximum history fibers
   give the exact finite-horizon two-port inequality;
3. their limsup growth gives a vector relative-fiber-entropy slack and a
   bidirectional subexponential exactness criterion;
4. the v0.20 clone is lifted to a finite one-class public-state quotient,
   proving that exact state bisimulation and finite class size do not control
   transcript-tree entropy; and
5. central counts, independent tries, multiplicity schedules, and mutations
   make each registered quantifier executable.
