## 1. Exact marker-family frontier

For `N` marker locations, at most `K` complete prover transcripts, at most `q`
adaptive ideal semantic queries, and public-coin zero-world soundness `s<1`,
v2.15-v2.16 prove the exact worst-marker completeness and gap:

```text
C*   = s + (1-s) min(1,Kq/N),
C*-s =     (1-s) min(1,Kq/N).
```

These formulas permit arbitrary finite round schedules.  Interaction compresses
to at most `K` all-zero-answer paths per public seed.  The cyclic public-coin
construction attains the upper bound, including exact rational soundness
mixtures.

The v2.1 honest `Find` cost remains `N` black-box probes before a marker-bearing
transcript can be formed.  Thus the family has matching Find, transcript, and
adaptive Check resource frontiers.

## 3. Why complete-resolution item 3 remains open

The v0.1 document asks a solution to provide both:

1. “a formal complexity class for the protocols above”; and
2. matching communication, semantic-query, and honest-prover lower bounds.

The first item is a requested deliverable, not an already frozen cross-task
class.  V0.1 does not choose:

- `WV-FIX` versus `WV-ADM` quantification;
- a uniform reduction notion between task families;
- which transcript encodings and structural side information reductions must
  preserve;
- whether resource bounds are worst-case, distributional, advice-relative, or
  oracle-relative across tasks; or
- the operational binding contract outside the repaired online subclass.

Consequently “the universal matching lower bound” has no determinate domain or
invariance requirement yet.  Choosing one inside a harness would define a
successor problem, not solve the unchanged v0.1 program.

This does not mean a cross-task theorem is impossible.  It means the next
theorem must start by registering its formal class and reductions, after which
its claim can be proved or refuted.
