## Audit conclusion

The repository now contains a self-contained candidate mathematical resolution
of the deterministic noiseless achieved-transcript object literally defined in
ASMP-4 v0.1, together with exact boundary fixtures. It is appropriate to stop
adding finite grids to that object unless a reviewer identifies a proof error
or chooses a different architecture/rate semantics. External mathematical and
scope review remain missing, so the result must continue to be labelled a
candidate rather than a peer-reviewed resolution.

The reason to stop is structural, not exhaustion. Under the frozen symmetric
complete-transcript cost and free serial computation, bilateral normal forms
copy either admitted port tree onto both interfaces without changing the plant
trajectory. No larger census can produce unequal optimal thresholds without
silently adding a new cost, deadline, syntax, computation, side-information,
or reliability coordinate. The v0.4 harness demonstrates exactly how such an
explicit addition restores a nonrectangular frontier.

## Frozen target and scope

The authoritative v0.1 target asks for the complete read/write capacity region
for nonlinear uncertain evaluator-relative confinement, with transversal and
finite-horizon terms. Its displayed canonical rates charge both ports by

~~~text
limsup_T log2 |M_q^C(T)| / T.
~~~

Variable-length expected or worst-prefix variants must be charged explicitly.
The architecture forbids uncharged side channels and requires memory, delays,
block coding, and plant-independent randomness to be registered.

The candidate resolution makes the corresponding operational conventions
explicit: realized transcript languages, universal zero-error safety,
relabelable finite port alphabets, arbitrary registered component computation,
fixed authority, and zero-delay or timing-compatible public fixed-FIFO
delivery. Shared plant-independent randomness derandomizes under either
unambiguous code-level universal zero-error convention. No claim is made for a
pointwise almost-sure, noisy, private-channel-state, deadline-incompatible, or
fixed peak-alphabet successor.
