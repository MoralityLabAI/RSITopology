# ASMP candidate-set hostile referee audit v0.1

## Verdict

The seven entries are coherent **proposed theorem frontiers**, but they should
not yet be advertised as an actual Millennium Prize list. This edition is
admissible as a problem-definition note because it states classical parent
fields, accepts negative resolutions, and exposes rather than hides its
definition debt. The graduation criteria in the main document are requirements
for a later public-prize version, not a claim already satisfied by v0.1.

An internal hostile pass found and repaired an undefined ASMP-6 capacity
argument, circular search content in ASMP-3's invariant, an unsupported generic
write-rate equality in ASMP-4, an undefined maximality order in ASMP-5, and an
oracular telemetry loophole plus undefined transformation sets in ASMP-7. These
repairs improve closure; they do not substitute for outside field review.

## Load-bearing cautions

### ASMP-1

Unrestricted causal alignment is vacuous and passive representation recovery is
not identifiable. The mechanism class, intervention family, abstraction, group
action, function-class entropy, conditioning, and effect margins are not
optional. A counterexample refutes the frozen Cut-Separation Conjecture;
resolving the broader classification program requires a replacement criterion
or an impossibility theorem.

### ASMP-2

No distribution-free safety generalization theorem is possible. Version 0.1
therefore limits tangent spanning to a transported semiparametric local
experiment and states global continuation separately. External review must
still test whether the registered quotient and conditioning modulus are the
right ones and whether the local-to-global target is sufficiently narrow.

### ASMP-3

Formal verification and interactive proofs already solve powerful syntactic
cases. The independent frontier is semantic/noisy judgment, efficient honest
refutation, and obfuscation. The atomic query language now bounds description
length, locality, and cost, but those syntactic bounds do not by themselves
prove that one query cannot encode the full task. The required
encoding-invariance theorem and the game still need complexity-theory review.

### ASMP-4

Output invariance entropy and data-rate theorems already exist. The new claim is
the joint split-port capacity region with no side channels and an
evaluator-transversal quotient. Version 0.1 no longer imports a write-rate
equality from the read channel. A genuinely nonrectangular or unequal-threshold
worked case is still needed to demonstrate liveness of the new part.

### ASMP-5

Arbitrary computable self-modification is too broad; undecidability is expected.
The target is the positive/negative frontier under a declared effective
reduction order; no unique greatest class is presumed. A frozen trusted kernel
makes some composition easy but may cap open-ended reflective change, while a
rewritable verifier reintroduces proof-theoretic obstacles.

### ASMP-6

Steganography, channel resolvability, and covert communication are mature. The
new content must come from endogenous learned cover distributions, restricted
learned monitors, active audits, and shared model/tool side information.
Qualitative noninjectivity is not enough for positive rate; the quantitative
resolving modulus must tensorize. Pairwise information statistics cannot
exclude synergistic collusion.

### ASMP-7

Statistical distinguishability itself is classical. The unresolved theorem is
minimum-cost transformation-closed telemetry under composition. Version 0.1
excludes a channel that simply queries the policy predicate and uses uniform
composite testing separation rather than pairwise orbit separation. A concrete
physically admissible access class is still required for a prize-ready version.

## Distinct-object and overlap check

- ASMP-1 identifies internal causal objects; ASMP-7 identifies institutionally
  attestable execution predicates. They are not the same quotient.
- ASMP-2 certifies generalization across environments; ASMP-3 certifies answers
  via interaction. Neither supplies the other's premises.
- ASMP-4 concerns live control-channel capacity; ASMP-7 concerns retrospective
  or concurrent telemetry evidence.
- ASMP-5 concerns certificates under self-replacement; ASMP-6 concerns hidden
  communication among multiple strategic agents.

These distinctions do not prove pairwise mathematical independence. ASMP-6 can
embed into ASMP-7 under a collusion policy predicate, interactive ASMP-7
telemetry can resemble ASMP-3, and ASMP-2 can be represented as a composite
experiment. A reduction theorem may justify merging a later version.

## Missing work before a public prize proposal

1. Commission field experts to audit prior art in causal identifiability,
   semiparametric theory, decentralized control, proof theory, semantic
   interactive proofs, covert communication, and statistical experiments.
2. Turn each parametric skeleton into a fully frozen canonical version whose
   quantifiers survive worked examples and adversarial encodings.
3. Prove small canonical positive and negative cases for every problem,
   including a genuinely split-port ASMP-4 example and non-oracular ASMP-7
   example.
4. Publish the definition note for external counterexamples before attaching
   money or prestige.
5. Establish a governance body independent of the problem authors to adjudicate
   amendments and resolutions.

## Referee conclusion

Version 0.1 succeeds as an audited candidate agenda: seven increasingly typed
frontiers, seven two-sided resolution policies, and no claim that synthetic or
model-specific evidence proves them. It does not yet establish that all seven
are public-prize-ready, uniquely correct, pairwise irreducible, or unsolved in
every nearby formalization. Those are matters for external mathematical review.
