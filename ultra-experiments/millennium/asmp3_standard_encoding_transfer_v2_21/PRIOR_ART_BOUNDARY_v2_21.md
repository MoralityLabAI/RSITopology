# ASMP-3 v2.21 prior-art boundary

No novelty is claimed for halting-problem undecidability, non-enumerability of
`NONHALT`, computable many-one reductions, transcript coupling, data
processing, total variation, minimax, or effective Gödel encodings.

The package-specific contribution is their source-faithful composition:

- the reduction image remains inside the declared noisy weak-verifier resource
  regime;
- the proof targets the full encoded membership set rather than relabelling its
  image as the target;
- representation robustness is stated as five auditable adequacy axioms; and
- a labelled-strategy coupling removes the `FIX`/`ADM` escape without assuming
  a particular number of rounds or message format.

This is an internal research artifact, not a comprehensive literature survey.
Before any originality or prize claim, experts should compare the result with
known undecidability theorems for game values, interactive proof systems, and
program-indexed semantic properties.
