# Prior-art boundary v0.33

The periodic-orbit, shadowing, invariance-entropy, and pressure ingredients are
classical.  No novelty is claimed for those results.

- Da Silva and Kawan,
  [Invariance Entropy of Hyperbolic Control Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.2416),
  derive matching entropy bounds for hyperbolic chain control sets under mild
  assumptions, using periodic trajectories, controllability, and shadowing.
  Their result is the closest nonlinear-control precedent for the safe-closing
  route.
- Colonius, Santana, and Cossich,
  [Invariance Pressure for Control Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03025),
  develop weighted invariance pressure and equivalence between spanning-control
  and invariant-cover formulations.
- Zhong, Huang, and Zou,
  [Invariance Entropy for Uncertain Control Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05510),
  treat uncertain-control branching trees and relate them to invariance
  feedback entropy.

The package's narrower contribution is an elementary ASMP-specific synthesis:
two separately charged transcript ports, component-indexed nonconvex regions,
vector closing overhead, and an aperiodic no-finite-quotient boundary.  It does
not claim a new shadowing theorem or a new formula for classical one-port
invariance entropy.
