# ASMP-3 current-harness stopping boundary v2.6

## Recommendation

**Stop extending the current parity, coupling, restart, and finite-Refute grids.**

They have already certified the exact formulas and sharp cases they were built
to test. More rows cannot clear any remaining blocker.

## Why more enumeration cannot help

1. **Normative definition.** A computation cannot decide whether v0.1 intended
   FIX, ADM, or an operationally binding `Refute`. That requires a normative
   repaired version or authoritative adjudication.
2. **Universal theorem.** The transparent normal form does not quantify over
   protocols lacking rejection witnesses or efficient ideal simulation. Closing
   that scope requires a new theorem or a separating positive interface, not a
   larger parameter grid.
3. **Universal lower bounds.** Exact unique-marker and one-message frontiers do
   not become lower bounds for every interactive protocol by increasing `N`.
4. **External reproduction.** Repository-generated receipts cannot establish
   real reviewer identity, expertise, or independence. The gate remains `0/2`.

## Legitimate resume triggers

Resume this line only when at least one of the following exists:

- a normative successor freezing the missing game and quantifier semantics;
- a genuinely broader normal-form theorem or counterexample;
- interface-uniform interactive resource lower bounds; or
- attributable independent expert reproduction of the resealed decisive packet.

This is a stopping boundary for the exhausted harness architecture, not a claim
that the broader research frontier has no remaining mathematics.
