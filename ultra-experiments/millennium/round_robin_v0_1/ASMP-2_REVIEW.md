# Round-robin review: ASMP-2 crossed-shift continuation

## Verdict

**`run_pilot`**, but label its successful outcome
`instrument_valid_forced_construction`, not empirical support for a general
shift certificate. Do not launch the optional sweep until its finite universe
is specified.

## Findings

### 1. The headline pass is forced by construction — `scope_narrowing`

This is the strongest objection. For the only distinct world pair,

```text
L_+(theta) - L_-(theta) = theta_1 theta_2 / 4.
```

Therefore the box supremum is arithmetically `t^2/4`, the axial source laws
coincide because the product vanishes there, and the diagonal distinguishes
the worlds because it does not. The enumerator can catch implementation errors
and emit a checkable counterexample, but the registered equalities cannot
surprise us. Under the collaboration protocol this is a forced finite
construction, not a discovered phase transition or evidence that the active
selector generalizes.

**Concrete repair:** make a short analytic proposition the primary artifact
and call the exact-rational runner its independent checker. Reserve an
`evidence_candidate` label for the optional active-design comparison, where
the winning source is not planted into a two-world family.

### 2. `kappa` is not fully reproducible — `repairable`

“After Fisher whitening” does not freeze the reference distribution, score
ordering, norm, whitening convention, or a numerical lower bound. The two
dedicated `Z_i` channels make positive rank plausible, but an independent
checker cannot reproduce the claimed conditioning margin from prose alone.
Freeze the exact Fisher matrix at `theta=0`, its risk-relevant quotient map,
and either an exact characteristic-polynomial certificate or a rational lower
bound for its least singular value.

### 3. The optional resource estimate is not yet auditable — `repairable`

Dimensions and polynomial degrees do not define how many source designs,
candidate environments, adversarial points, or selector steps are evaluated.
“Enumerate or interval-bound” also hides two different complexities. The
`2-6 h`, 8-core estimate is credible for a bounded matrix suite, but not for
enumerating arbitrary design subsets. Freeze these counts and give an atomic
work-unit estimate before scale-up.

### 4. Liveness and controls — `nonissue`

The crossed pair supplies nonzero quadratic ambiguity; the affine control has
none; the unspanned arm supplies a first-order failure; and risk-null,
abstention, coordinate, and count-matched controls isolate the intended
mechanism. Exact rational arithmetic makes the `<1 min`, `<256 MB`, CPU-only
pilot estimate credible. The safety-threshold example is also numerically
nonvacuous (`0.75 > 0.60 > 0.50`).

## Claim limit

A pass proves one explicit counterexample to “local tangent span implies
global safety.” It does not establish necessary or sufficient shift-spanning
conditions, sample complexity, or a useful general source-selection policy.
