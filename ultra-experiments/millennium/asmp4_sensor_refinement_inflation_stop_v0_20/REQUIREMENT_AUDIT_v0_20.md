# ASMP-4 requirement audit at v0.20

## Audit question

Does the current repository resolve frozen ASMP-4, or does it provide a
convincing harness-backed reason to stop the present local-fixture program?

## Frozen resolution requirements

### 1. Coordinate-invariant read and write transversal entropies

**Verdict: not achieved globally.** V0.20 proves that forced-raw read entropy
changes by `log2(m)` under irrelevant registered color refinements while the
plant, evaluator, safety policy, and writes are fixed. A coordinate-invariant
definition must either include the sensor experiment as instance data,
optimize over encoders, or quotient irrelevant labels. The source does not
freeze one of those three choices in its formal definition.

### 2. Variational formula for the entire achievable region

**Verdict: achieved only for the declared local family.** V0.18 and v0.19 give
the exact finite-transducer formula
`[1+log2(rho(A_I)),infinity) x [2,infinity)` after a sensor experiment and
initial belief are registered. No repository artifact proves this formula for
the source's full nonlinear normally hyperbolic class.

### 3. Converse and constructive schemes

**Verdict: achieved locally, missing globally.** The positive-volume collar
chain proves current-`q` decoding necessity, normal expansion and write lower
bounds, and matching controllers. Those arguments do not constitute a global
nonlinear variational converse.

### 4. Exact finite-horizon margin corrections

**Verdict: achieved locally.** Across the registered sensor families, the
normal spanning number is exactly `ceil(rho*2^T)`, and read/write products are
verified independently. No universal nonlinear finite-margin theorem is
claimed.

### 5. Boundaries of partial observability, uncertainty, nonhyperbolicity, and
side information

**Verdict: substantial exact boundary evidence, not exhaustive global
closure.** V0.16-v0.20 classify deterministic partitions, stochastic supports,
finite hidden sensor state, initial-state uncertainty, and irrelevant raw
refinement. Continuous observations, general nonlinear coupling, delays,
block error, and other declared architectures remain outside the harness.

## Semantic audit

The source's capacity region quantifies over registered causal codes, which is
consistent with optimizing the sensor encoder. Its C19 split-port witness also
says to hold the sensor experiment fixed, which treats that experiment as
registered instance data. V0.15 therefore correctly rejected a claim that
sensor parameterization is semantically absent. Seven other clauses remain
ambiguous about the class grammar and quotient rule.

Consequently, raw-refinement inflation does not refute the broad frozen
classification program. It refutes only the claim that a plant/evaluator pair
alone fixes a forced-raw read number across unspecified sensor experiments.

## Operational stopping decision

**Stop the present local-fixture program.** The decision is supported by:

- a general symbolic clone theorem;
- exact computed and golden-language fixtures;
- a complete 768-instance clone census in two implementations;
- distinct deterministic robustness ensembles covering larger nondeterministic
  belief graphs;
- exact coarsening and finite-margin checks;
- five mutation kills; and
- a current-state 21-package regression of 244 tests.

Another favorable local plant or sensor does not answer the missing semantic
and global-theory questions. It can only instantiate one more point after the
registry has been chosen.

## Resume conditions

Resume ASMP-4 work when at least one of the following is supplied:

1. an external adjudication fixing sensor-parameterized, encoder-optimized, or
   quotient-first semantics;
2. a formal grammar for the global normally hyperbolic registered class,
   including observation access and allowed sensor refinements; or
3. a proposed global nonlinear variational theorem whose hypotheses and
   invariance claims can be attacked by the existing harness.

Until then, the evidence supports a convincing operational stop, not a claim
of complete mathematical resolution.

## V0.21 successor note

V0.21 satisfies the local exact-support quotient-first resume route: duplicate
raw labels are removed by their full event-incidence signatures. It does not
satisfy the global resume conditions above because it neither proves
control-minimality nor fixes the nonlinear registered-class grammar.

local sensor class under same-step observation and unrestricted finite encoder
memory. This satisfies another local resume route, but not the global
normally-hyperbolic variational obligations.

V0.23 then isolates the load-bearing timing datum: the local same-step region
becomes empty at every positive integer observation delay for arbitrary modes,
while charged current preview restores it. This closes one local delay
boundary but still does not supply the global nonlinear variational theorem or
fix the source-wide registration grammar.

V0.24 subsequently supplies a parameterized support-functional formula for
every reset-concatenable registered block class. It is a genuine abstract
advance beyond another fixture and exactly recovers the nonrectangular v0.7
wedge. Its explicit periodic block-completeness hypothesis prevents promotion
to an unspecified nonresetting global class, so the original global resume
conditions are narrowed but not discharged.

V0.25 proves the v0.24 periodic-completeness hypothesis for finite public
additive scheduler graphs inside each reachable recurrent SCC. An irreversible
fork shows that several recurrent SCCs require a disjunction rather than a
single convex support family. The remaining global gap is therefore finite
sufficient-state/additive abstraction for the declared nonlinear class, not
another finite sensor fixture.

V0.26 proves the missing region-transfer implication for every supplied exact
costed alternating-bisimulation quotient, including adversarial successor
sets. Its aperiodic Thue-Morse witness also shows that a finite exact quotient
cannot be made a necessary condition for existence of an asymptotic rate. The
remaining global gap is quotient construction or a more general replacement,
not another bounded registration census.

V0.27 proves robust region transfer for an epsilon-cost alternating relation
with exact safety and gives a strict Lipschitz-margin route from observation

boundary witness still flips the region from nonempty to empty when the margin
is omitted. This strengthens rather than relaxes the stop on unguarded global
approximation claims and repeated bounded fixtures.

V0.28 lifts this package's clone obstruction into a general quotient theorem.
