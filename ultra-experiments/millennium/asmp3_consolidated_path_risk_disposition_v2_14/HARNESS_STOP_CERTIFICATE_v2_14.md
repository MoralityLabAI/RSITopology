and correlated-noise grids.  This is a scope decision backed by exact harness
evidence, not a claim that unrestricted ASMP-3 is solved.

cannot enlarge the quantified theorem or change a requirement status.

## Legitimate next triggers

Resume the mathematical program only for one of these concrete events:

1. an authoritative normative interface adoption;
2. a concrete task-specific non-black-box family or a universal structure
   theorem;
3. an interface-uniform interactive resource lower-bound theorem; or
4. attributable external reproduction.

Items 2 and 3 are genuine new theorem lanes and can be pursued locally when a
specific formal target is registered.  Items 1 and 4 require authority or
identity evidence that a repository cannot manufacture.

## Safe stopping conclusion

The current harness is sufficient to justify stopping the exhausted finite
families.  It is insufficient to justify “fully solved,” because it does not
quantify over all task-specific non-black-box protocols, provide universal
interactive resource lower bounds, select the normative successor, or satisfy
external review.
