# Two structural engines in the ASMP experiment portfolio

## 1. Structured observation kernels

ASMP-1 interaction order, ASMP-9 cycle queries, and ASMP-10 derivative jets
share a rank-filtration structure: an observation map has a structured kernel,
identifiability changes at a rank threshold, and a witness occupies the first
invisible graded piece. The existing motif document deliberately classifies
this as a family of structured classical rank theorems, not one deep new law.

## 2. Equilibrium survival under expanding deviations

ASMP-12 is different. There is no observation kernel and no one-order witness.
A larger resource budget enlarges both the program set and the deviation set;
the equilibrium correspondence can gain or lose profiles. The organizing
objects are deviation graphs, survival regions, and birth/death events under
resource and payoff variation.

## Portfolio interpretation

The portfolio is therefore not one theorem template repeated everywhere. It
uses two mathematical engines already native to RSITopology:

- identifiability through structured filtrations; and
- robustness through equilibrium/section persistence under changing admissible
  structure.

Their coherence is methodological: both ask what remains well-defined as an
interface is enlarged or transported, while their failure mechanisms and proof
obligations remain distinct.
