# ASMP-4 global stopping disposition at v0.23

## Decision

Stop adding local ASMP-4 fixtures. Do not claim that the canonical global
problem is mathematically resolved. The repository has closed a substantial
finite local collar family, but the remaining step requires a formal global
registered-class grammar or an external semantic decision that is not present
in the canonical source.

## Harness basis

The stopping decision is supported by 24 explicit ASMP-4 packages and a clean
274-test regression in 296.96 seconds. Central and import-independent
implementations agree on the following exact forks:

1. V0.20 proves that irrelevant raw-label cloning leaves the plant, evaluator,
   feasibility, and write requirement fixed while shifting forced-raw read
   rate by `log2(m)` without bound.
2. V0.21 removes duplicate-label inflation under a declared support-incidence
   quotient, but does not prove that this quotient is globally control-minimal
   or selected by the source.
3. V0.22 proves that unrestricted same-step causal encoding collapses every
   feasible finite local sensor to `[2,infinity) x [2,infinity)`.
4. V0.23 proves that the same local plant has empty region at every positive
   integer delay without current preview, returns to
   `[2,infinity) x [2,infinity)` with charged current preview, and has
   `[1,infinity) x [1,infinity)` for a charged pre-safety constant-mode seed.
5. Earlier exact packages give rectangular, unequal-threshold, and genuinely
   nonrectangular split-port regions, positive-dimensional normally hyperbolic
   geometry, positive-volume collars, matching converses, constructive
   policies, and exact finite-horizon ceiling corrections.

These are not sampling-based observations. The decisive witnesses are exact
symbolic interval, language, spectral-radius, and finite-census statements.

## Canonical requirement audit

| Canonical requirement | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Coordinate-invariant read/write transversal quantities | Closed for declared local contracts; not defined for the ungrammatized global registered class. |
| Entire achievable region | Exact for the finite local families; not a formula for every nonlinear normally hyperbolic registration. |
| Converse and construction | Matching locally; no global nonlinear theorem. |
| Finite-horizon correction | Exact `ceil(rho 2^T)` locally; no global correction theorem. |
| Boundary counterexamples | Extensive exact sensor, uncertainty, memory, refinement, timing, preview, and randomness boundaries; continuous/global closure remains open. |

The source says that sensor, controller, actuator, delays, block coding,
memory, and shared randomness are explicit architecture data, but it does not
provide a formal grammar over those registrations for the requested universal
classification. Nor does it choose forced-raw, quotient-first, or
encoder-optimized sensor semantics. The split-port example is consistent with
parameterization, so this is a reason to stop unbounded local enumeration, not
a counterexample to every possible formal repair.

## Why further autonomous work should stop

Another bounded plant, sensor, delay, or decoder adds one point after a
registration has been selected. It cannot determine which registration class
the canonical problem intended, and it cannot turn the phrase “registered
normally hyperbolic, locally controllable class” into a quantified mathematical
grammar. Choosing that grammar autonomously would change the problem rather
than solve it.

## Resume conditions

Resume only when at least one of these is supplied:

1. a formal global grammar specifying admissible plants, sensors, causal
   encoders, delays, memory, block codes, randomness quantifiers, actuator
   decoders, and quotient equivalence;
2. an external adjudication selecting fixed-sensor, quotient-first, or
   encoder-optimized semantics and the exact timing contract;
3. a concrete global nonlinear variational formula with hypotheses precise
   enough for the existing harness to prove or falsify; or
4. attributable expert review mapping the canonical wording to an established
   formal class and explaining how the two charged ports extend prior theory.

Until one of these occurs, the evidence supports a convincing harness-backed
operational stop and explicitly does not support a full-resolution claim.

## V0.24 successor note

V0.24 satisfies part of resume condition 3 without choosing a hidden canonical
semantics. It proves that a concave weighted support family reconstructs the
entire region generated by reset-concatenable safe blocks and recovers the
earlier rectangular, wedge, refinement, and delay cases. Promotion to the full
infinite-horizon region is conditional on periodic block completeness. Thus
the abstract resettable variational lane is now productive, while the stop on
unbounded local fixtures and ungrammatized nonresetting global claims remains.

V0.25 further proves periodic completeness for finite public additive
scheduler state and locates a nonconvex irreversible-SCC boundary. This narrows
the open global question to existence of a suitable finite/additive public
abstraction (or a replacement for it) in nonlinear and infinite-belief
registrations; it does not justify returning to unbounded local enumeration.

V0.26 proves that any exact costed alternating-bisimulation quotient preserves
the complete worst-path vector budget region, so a deterministic finite
quotient may invoke v0.25. The Thue-Morse counterboundary shows that finite
exact quotients do not characterize every system with a well-defined rate.
The stop therefore remains on unregistered global quotient existence and on
further local fixture enumeration.

V0.27 allows matched additive edge costs to differ by `epsilon`, with sharp
asymptotic budget slack `epsilon`, provided safety and alternating successor
matching stay exact. Its arbitrarily-close safe/unsafe loops show why metric
closeness alone cannot reopen a zero-error global claim; a strict registered
safety margin is required. The productive route remains a global abstraction
or variational theorem with those guards, not more local sensor fixtures.

V0.28 identifies the additional invariant needed when the charged object is a
whole port language rather than an additive path: maximum causal transcript
fiber growth. A finite one-class quotient can hide exponential fibers, while
subexponential fibers preserve the rate. This again narrows a global theorem
condition without justifying further local fixture enumeration.

V0.29 shows that prefix branching needs a stricter local invariant than v0.28
terminal fibers: successor-fiber multiplicities accumulated along causal paths.
An injective terminal map can still carry positive local branch-fiber entropy.
This is another global factor condition, not a reason to resume local sensor
enumeration.
